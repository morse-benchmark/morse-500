The video displays a sequence of geometric shapes and numbers that appear to be associated with the shapes. The shapes shown are a triangle, a cube, a sphere, a square pyramid, and a cylinder. The numbers that appear before and after these shapes are:

- Before cube: 1.00
- After square pyramid: 1.00

The instruction is to sum up the numbers before the cube and after the square pyramid.

So, we sum 1.00 + 1.00 = 2.00.

Rounding to 2 decimal places, the result is still 2.00.
