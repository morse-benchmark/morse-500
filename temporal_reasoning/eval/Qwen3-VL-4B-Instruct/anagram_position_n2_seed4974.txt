We are given the word "BEAUTIFUL" and asked to find the position of the letter 'F' after a "shuffle 2".

First, let's understand what "shuffle 2" means. In the context of such puzzles, "shuffle 2" typically means taking the first 2 letters and moving them to the end of the string. So, we take the first 2 letters of "BEAUTIFUL" and append them to the end.

Original word: "BEAUTIFUL"

First 2 letters: "BE"

Remaining letters: "AUTIFUL"

After shuffle 2: "AUTIFUL" + "BE" = "AUTIFULBE"

Now, we need to find the position of the letter 'F' in this new string "AUTIFULBE".

Let's list the positions (0-indexed):

- Position 0: 'A'
- Position 1: 'U'
- Position 2: 'T'
- Position 3: 'I'
- Position 4: 'F' ← This is the letter 'F'
- Position 5: 'U'
- Position 6: 'L'
- Position 7: 'B'

The letter 'F' is at position 4.
