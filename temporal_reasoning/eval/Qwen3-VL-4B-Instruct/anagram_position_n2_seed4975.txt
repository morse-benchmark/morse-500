We are given the word "MARYLAND" and asked to find the position of the letter 'M' after a "shuffle 2".

First, let's understand what "shuffle 2" means. In many contexts, especially in puzzles or riddles, "shuffle 2" typically means taking the first 2 letters and moving them to the end of the string. So, we take the first two letters of "MARYLAND", which are "MA", and move them to the end.

Original string: M A R Y L A N D

After shuffle 2: We take the first 2 letters "MA" and move them to the end.

So, the new string becomes: R Y L A N D M A

Now, we need to find the position of the letter 'M' in this new string.

Let's list the positions (0-indexed):

- Position 0: R
- Position 1: Y
- Position 2: L
- Position 3: A
- Position 4: N
- Position 5: D
- Position 6: M ← This is the 'M'
- Position 7: A

So, the letter 'M' is at position 6.

Therefore, the answer is 6.
