We are given a video that shows a ball traveling through a series of vertical bars. The ball starts on the left and moves to the right, hitting each bar in sequence. The question asks: "How many seconds did the ball travel BEFORE hitting the 3rd object?"

We need to determine the time it takes for the ball to travel from its starting position to the 3rd bar.

From the video, we can observe the following:

- At 0.0 seconds, the ball is at the leftmost position, just before the first bar.
- At 0.3 seconds, the ball hits the first bar.
- At 0.6 seconds, the ball hits the second bar.
- At 0.9 seconds, the ball hits the third bar.

Therefore, the ball travels for 0.9 seconds before hitting the 3rd object.

The answer is: 0.9 seconds.

\boxed{0.9}
