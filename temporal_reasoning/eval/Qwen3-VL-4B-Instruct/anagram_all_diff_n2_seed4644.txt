We are given a sequence of words: "KEYBOARD", "ADYBERKO", "OEKARYDB". We are to find the sum of the distance of all shifts that happened to the letter 'D' across all word moves.

First, let's understand what is meant by "distance of all shifts that happened to the letter 'D' across all word moves".

This likely refers to the number of positions the letter 'D' moves from its original position in the word to its new position in the next word. Since the words are given in sequence, we can assume that each word is a transformation of the previous one, and we are to calculate the shift of the 'D' from one word to the next.

But looking at the words:

- "KEYBOARD" → "ADYBERKO" → "OEKARYDB"

We need to find the position of 'D' in each word, then calculate the distance (i.e., the absolute difference in positions) between consecutive words.

Let's index the letters in each word (starting from 0):

1. "KEYBOARD"
   - K(0), E(1), Y(2), B(3), O(4), A(5), R(6), D(7) → 'D' is at position 7.

2. "ADYBERKO"
   - A(0), D(1), Y(2), B(3), E(4), R(5), K(6), O(7) → 'D' is at position 1.

3. "OEKARYDB"
   - O(0), E(1), K(2), A(3), R(4), Y(5), D(6), B(7) → 'D' is at position 6.

Now, calculate the shifts:

- From "KEYBOARD" to "ADYBERKO": 'D' moves from position 7 to position 1. The distance is |7 - 1| = 6.

- From "ADYBERKO" to "OEKARYDB": 'D' moves from position 1 to position 6. The distance is |1 - 6| = 5.

Sum of all shifts: 6 + 5 = 11.

Therefore, the answer is 11.
