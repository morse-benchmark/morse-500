We are given a sequence of word moves, starting with "COMPUTER" and ending with "ERMOCUTP". The question asks for the sum of the distance of all shifts that happened to the letter 'R' across all word moves.

Let’s break this down step by step.

---

**Step 1: Understand the word moves**

The word "COMPUTER" is transformed into "OCUEPTRM", then to "ERMOCUTP".

We need to track the position of the letter 'R' in each word and calculate how far it has moved (in terms of distance) from its original position.

---

**Step 2: Original word: "COMPUTER"**

Positions (0-indexed):

C O M P U T E R  
0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7

The letter 'R' is at position 7.

---

**Step 3: First transformation: "COMPUTER" → "OCUEPTRM"**

Let’s write the new word:

O C U E P T R M  
0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7

Now, 'R' is at position 6.

So, the shift from position 7 → position 6 is a shift of **-1** (i.e., moved left by 1 position).

---

**Step 4: Second transformation: "OCUEPTRM" → "ERMOCUTP"**

New word:

E R M O C U T P  
0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7

Now, 'R' is at position 1.

So, the shift from position 6 → position 1 is a shift of **-5** (i.e., moved left by 5 positions).

---

**Step 5: Sum the distances of all shifts**

We have two shifts:

- First shift: -1
- Second shift: -5

Sum = -1 + (-5) = **-6**

But the question asks for the **sum of the distance** of all shifts.

“Distance” typically refers to the absolute value of the shift, regardless of direction.

So, we take absolute values:

- | -1 | = 1
- | -5 | = 5

Sum = 1 + 5 = **6**

---

**Final Answer:**

\boxed{6}
