5. Then, the arrow points from the white circle to the red hexagon (at the top right).

Now, we need to find which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path.

Let’s calculate the distances between consecutive shapes:

- Yellow square to blue circle: relatively close.
- Blue circle to orange pentagon: relatively close.
- Orange pentagon to white circle: relatively far (since white circle is at the center, and orange pentagon is at the bottom left).
- White circle to red hexagon: relatively close (red hexagon is at the top right, close to the center).
