We are given a sequence of five cubes, each rolling and showing a different color on top. The task is to count how many times the color "yellow" appears on top after all the rolls.

Let’s go step by step:

1. **Initial positions (before any rolls):**
   - Cube 1: Top face is white.
   - Cube 2: Top face is purple.
   - Cube 3: Top face is yellow.
   - Cube 4: Top face is red.
   - Cube 5: Top face is white.

2. **After the first roll (cube 1):**
   - The cube rolls and now shows purple on top.

3. **After the second roll (cube 2):**
   - The cube rolls and now shows yellow on top.

4. **After the third roll (cube 3):**
   - The cube rolls and now shows green on top.

5. **After the fourth roll (cube 4):**
   - The cube rolls and now shows white on top.

6. **After the fifth roll (cube 5):**
   - The cube rolls and now shows blue on top.

Now, we need to count how many times "yellow" appears on top after all the rolls.

From the above, yellow appears only once — after the second roll.

Therefore, the total number of times yellow was rolled is:

\[
\boxed{1}
