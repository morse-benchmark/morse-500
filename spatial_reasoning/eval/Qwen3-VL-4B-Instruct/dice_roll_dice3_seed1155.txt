9. Ninth roll: The top faces are blue, green, and yellow.
10. Tenth roll: The top faces are white, red, and purple.

- Blue: appears in rolls 1, 3, 7, 9 → 4 times
- Red: appears in rolls 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 → 5 times
- White: appears in rolls 1, 4, 6, 8 → 4 times
- Green: appears in rolls 3, 6, 9 → 3 times
- Yellow: appears in rolls 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 → 6 times
