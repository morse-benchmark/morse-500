We are given a cube with faces colored blue, red, green, yellow, orange, and purple. The cube is shown rotating and moving along a path made of black squares. The cube starts with a red face on top, then moves to a yellow face, then to a purple face, then to an orange face, then to a green face, then to a blue face, and finally to a red face. The question is asking what color face will be on top once the cube reaches the red square. Since the cube is moving along the path and the red square is the last square in the path, the cube will have a red face on top when it reaches the red square. Therefore, the answer is red.
