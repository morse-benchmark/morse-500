We are given a sequence of four cubes, each rolling and showing a different color on top. We need to count how many times the color yellow appears on top.

Let’s analyze each cube’s top face after rolling:

1. **First cube**: Initially, the top face is blue. After rolling, the top face becomes red. So, blue is not yellow, and red is not yellow.
2. **Second cube**: Initially, the top face is green. After rolling, the top face becomes white. So, green is not yellow, and white is not yellow.
3. **Third cube**: Initially, the top face is purple. After rolling, the top face becomes yellow. So, purple is not yellow, but yellow is now on top.
4. **Fourth cube**: Initially, the top face is cyan (a shade of blue). After rolling, the top face becomes white. So, cyan is not yellow, and white is not yellow.

Now, we need to check if any other rolls resulted in yellow. Let’s go back to the sequence of rolls:

- First cube: top face changes from blue to red → no yellow.
- Second cube: top face changes from green to white → no yellow.
- Third cube: top face changes from purple to yellow → **yellow appears here**.
- Fourth cube: top face changes from cyan to white → no yellow.

Thus, yellow appears only once — on the third cube.
