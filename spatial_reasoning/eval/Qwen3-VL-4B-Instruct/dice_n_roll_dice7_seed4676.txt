We are given a sequence of six cubes, each with a different color on top after being rolled. The colors shown on top of the cubes are:

1. First cube: blue
2. Second cube: red
3. Third cube: white
4. Fourth cube: yellow
5. Fifth cube: green
6. Sixth cube: green

We are asked to count how many times "green" was rolled in total.

From the sequence above, we can see that "green" appears on the fifth and sixth cubes. Therefore, green was rolled 2 times.

Final Answer: $\boxed{2}$
