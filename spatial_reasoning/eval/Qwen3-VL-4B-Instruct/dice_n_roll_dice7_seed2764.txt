We are given a sequence of six cubes, each rolling and showing a different color on top. We are to count how many times the color "blue" appears on top.

First, let’s list the color that appears on top of each cube after rolling:

1. First cube: The top face is **red**.
2. Second cube: The top face is **green**.
3. Third cube: The top face is **blue**.
4. Fourth cube: The top face is **white**.
5. Fifth cube: The top face is **purple**.
6. Sixth cube: The top face is **yellow**.

Now, we look for the color "blue" in this list. We see that "blue" appears only once — on the third cube.

Therefore, the total number of times blue was rolled is 1.
