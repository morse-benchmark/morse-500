- The yellow square starts at the left side of the screen.
- The yellow square moves to the right, passing the purple square.
- The yellow square then moves to the left, passing the white triangle.
- The yellow square then moves to the right, passing the green triangle.

Step 2: Determine the furthest shape from the previous shape in the path.
- The yellow square is the furthest from the previous shape (the purple square) because it moves to the right and then to the left, passing the white triangle and the green triangle.
