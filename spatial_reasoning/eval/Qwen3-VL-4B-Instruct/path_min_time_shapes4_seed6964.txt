We are given a sequence of shapes moving from one to another, and we need to determine which color shape took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape. The key is to observe the trajectory of the arrows, which indicate the direction and path of movement.

Step 1: Identify the shapes and their colors.
- The shapes are: a blue square, a red circle, an orange triangle, a yellow square, a purple triangle, and a white circle.
- The arrows indicate the movement from one shape to the next.

Step 2: Observe the trajectory of the arrows.
- The first arrow moves from the blue square to the red circle.
- The second arrow moves from the red circle to the orange triangle.
- The third arrow moves from the orange triangle to the yellow square.
- The fourth arrow moves from the yellow square to the purple triangle.
- The fifth arrow moves from the purple triangle to the white circle.

Step 3: Determine the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape.
- The shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape is the one with the shortest distance traveled.
- The distance traveled is proportional to the length of the arrow.
- The shortest arrow is the one from the yellow square to the purple triangle.

Step 4: Identify the color of the shape that took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape.
- The shape that took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape is the purple triangle.

Therefore, the answer is purple.
