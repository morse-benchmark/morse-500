We are given a sequence of cubes that are rotating, and then asked to identify which net (a 2D pattern that folds into a cube) corresponds to the cube shown in the video.

Step 1: Observe the cube shown in the video.
The cube shown in the video has the following visible faces (from the perspective of the camera):
- Top face: Yellow
- Front face: Purple
- Right face: Light blue
- Left face: Red
- Back face: Not visible
- Bottom face: Not visible

However, since the cube is rotating, we need to consider the relative positions of the colors. The key is to identify which net, when folded, would result in the cube shown in the video.

Step 2: Analyze the nets.
We have four options: A, B, C, D.

Let’s examine each net:

Option A:
- The net has a central square with squares attached to its top, bottom, left, and right.
- The colors are: top: cyan, bottom: purple, left: yellow, right: red.
- When folded, the top face becomes the top of the cube, the bottom face becomes the bottom, etc.
- The yellow face would be on the left, red on the right, cyan on top, purple on bottom.
- This does not match the cube shown in the video.

Option B:
- The net has a central square with squares attached to its top, bottom, left, and right.
- The colors are: top: purple, bottom: red, left: yellow, right: yellow.
- When folded, the top face becomes the top of the cube, the bottom face becomes the bottom, etc.
- The yellow face would be on the left and right, which is not possible in a cube (only one face can be on each side).
- This does not match the cube shown in the video.

Option C:
- The net has a central square with squares attached to its top, bottom, left, and right.
- The colors are: top: cyan, bottom: yellow, left: red, right: yellow.
- When folded, the top face becomes the top of the cube, the bottom face becomes the bottom, etc.
- The yellow face would be on the top and bottom, which is not possible in a cube (only one face can be on each side).
- This does not match the cube shown in the video.

Option D:
- The net has a central square with squares attached to its top, bottom, left, and right.
- The colors are: top: yellow, bottom: purple, left: red, right: cyan.
- When folded, the top face becomes the top of the cube, the bottom face becomes the bottom, etc.
- The yellow face would be on the top, purple on the bottom, red on the left, cyan on the right.
- This matches the cube shown in the video.

Therefore, the correct answer is Option D.
