We are given a sequence of four cubes, each rolling and changing color on top. We need to count how many times the color red appears on top after all rolls.

Let's analyze each cube's top color after rolling:

1. **First cube**: Initially, the top is blue. After rolling, the top becomes red. So, red appears once here.
2. **Second cube**: Initially, the top is green. After rolling, the top becomes yellow. So, red does not appear here.
3. **Third cube**: Initially, the top is yellow. After rolling, the top becomes white. So, red does not appear here.
4. **Fourth cube**: Initially, the top is purple. After rolling, the top becomes red. So, red appears once here.

Adding up the occurrences: 1 (from the first cube) + 0 (from the second cube) + 0 (from the third cube) + 1 (from the fourth cube) = 2.

Thus, the total number of times red was rolled is 2.
