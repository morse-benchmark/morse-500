1. **First cube**: The visible faces are red, blue, and green. The hidden faces are white, yellow, and purple. So, one white face is hidden.

2. **Second cube**: The visible faces are yellow, blue, and green. The hidden faces are red, white, and purple. So, one white face is hidden.

3. **Third cube**: The visible faces are yellow, blue, and white. The hidden faces are red, green, and purple. So, no white faces are hidden.

Adding up the hidden white faces from all three cubes: 1 (from the first cube) + 1 (from the second cube) + 0 (from the third cube) = 2.

Therefore, the number of white faces currently hidden from the camera is 2.
