We are given a set of four cubes, each with colored faces. The color names and their corresponding colors are:

- blue → cyan (light blue)
- red → red
- white → white
- green → green
- yellow → yellow
- purple → purple

We need to count how many purple faces are currently hidden from the camera.

Let’s examine each cube one by one:

1. **First cube (leftmost)**:
   - The visible faces are: red (front), blue (top), and white (right).
   - The hidden faces are: green (back), yellow (bottom), and purple (left).
   - So, **1 purple face** is hidden.

2. **Second cube**:
   - The visible faces are: blue (front), yellow (top), and white (right).
   - The hidden faces are: red (back), green (bottom), and purple (left).
   - So, **1 purple face** is hidden.

3. **Third cube**:
   - The visible faces are: green (front), blue (top), and red (right).
   - The hidden faces are: white (back), yellow (bottom), and purple (left).
   - So, **1 purple face** is hidden.

4. **Fourth cube (rightmost)**:
   - The visible faces are: green (front), white (top), and red (right).
   - The hidden faces are: blue (back), yellow (bottom), and purple (left).
   - So, **1 purple face** is hidden.

Adding them up: 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4.

Thus, the total number of purple faces currently hidden from the camera is 4.
