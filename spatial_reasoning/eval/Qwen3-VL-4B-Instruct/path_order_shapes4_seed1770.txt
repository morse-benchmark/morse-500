The video begins by instructing the viewer to remember certain color names associated with specific shapes. The colors and their corresponding shapes are: blue (circle), red (circle), orange (circle), green (square), yellow (square), purple (square), and white (square). The viewer is then asked to observe the trajectory of the arrow, which indicates the path that will be followed to visit the shapes.

As the arrow moves, it first points to the green square, which is the first shape to be visited. The arrow then moves to the purple square, followed by the orange circle, and finally the blue circle.

Therefore, the color of the 1st shape to be visited is green.
