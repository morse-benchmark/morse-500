2. **Second roll:**
   - The cube shows a **green** face on top.
   - So, white is **not** shown here.

3. **Third roll:**
   - The cube shows a **blue** face on top.
   - So, white is **not** shown here.

4. **Fourth roll:**
   - The cube shows a **white** face on top.
