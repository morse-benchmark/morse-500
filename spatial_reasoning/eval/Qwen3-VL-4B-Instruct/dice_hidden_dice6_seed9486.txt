We are given a sequence of cubes, each with multiple colored faces. The goal is to count how many white faces are currently hidden from the camera.

Step 1: Understand the color mapping.
From the video, we are told:
- Blue → blue
- Red → red
- White → white
- Green → green
- Yellow → yellow
- Purple → purple

Step 2: Observe the cubes.
The cubes are shown in a row, each with different colored faces visible. We need to identify which faces are white and which are hidden.
