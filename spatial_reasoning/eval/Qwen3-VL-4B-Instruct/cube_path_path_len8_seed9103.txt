We are given a cube with colored faces: blue, red, green, yellow, orange, and purple. The cube is shown rotating and moving along a path made of black squares, with colored squares at certain positions. The cube starts with a red face on top, then moves to a yellow face, then to a purple face, then to a green face, then to a blue face, then to an orange face, and finally to a red face. The question asks what color face will be on top once the cube reaches the red square. Since the cube is moving along the path, and the red square is the last one in the sequence, the color face on top will be the one that was on top when the cube reached the red square. Looking at the sequence, the cube reaches the red square with a blue face on top. Therefore, the answer is blue.
