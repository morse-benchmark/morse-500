To find the surface area of the figure, we need to observe the structure and count the number of unit squares on its surface.

### Step 1: Understand the structure
The figure is composed of unit cubes arranged in a 3D structure. From the video, we can see that it is a 2x2x2 cube with one corner cube missing. This structure is often called a "corner cube" or "missing corner cube" structure.

### Step 2: Count the exposed faces
Each unit cube has 6 faces. However, when cubes are adjacent, their touching faces are not part of the surface area.

In a 2x2x2 cube, there are 8 unit cubes. If the cube were complete, the surface area would be:
- Each face of the 2x2x2 cube is 2x2 = 4 unit squares.
- There are 6 faces, so total surface area = 6 * 4 = 24 unit squares.

However, since one corner cube is missing, we need to adjust for the missing cube and the new exposed faces.

### Step 3: Adjust for the missing cube
When we remove a corner cube from a 2x2x2 cube, we:
- Lose 3 faces that were previously exposed (the faces that were touching the adjacent cubes).
- Gain 3 new faces that were previously internal (the faces that were touching the adjacent cubes).

So, the total surface area remains the same as the complete 2x2x2 cube.

### Step 4: Verify by counting exposed faces
Let's count the exposed faces directly:
- Each of the 8 cubes has 6 faces, so total faces = 8 * 6 = 48.
- Each adjacent pair of cubes shares 2 faces (since they are touching along edges), and there are 12 edges in a 2x2x2 cube, so total shared faces = 12 * 2 = 24.
- Therefore, total exposed faces = 48 - 24 = 24.

### Step 5: Conclusion
The surface area of the figure is 24 unit squares.
