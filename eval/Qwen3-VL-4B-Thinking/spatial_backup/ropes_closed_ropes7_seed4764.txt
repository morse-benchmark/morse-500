The video shows **7 straight lines** drawn in different colors (white, purple, red, orange, yellow, green, and blue), with **no two lines parallel** and **no three lines intersecting at a single point**. This is a classic **general position** configuration for lines in a plane.

In such a configuration, the number of **bounded regions** (i.e., **closed shapes**) is given by the formula:

$$
\text{Number of closed shapes} = \frac{(n - 1)(n - 2)}{2}
$$

Where $ n $ is the number of lines.

$$
\text{Number of closed shapes} = \frac{(7 - 1)(7 - 2)}{2} = \frac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15
$$

This formula is derived from Euler's formula for planar graphs and is valid when all lines intersect in general position (i.e., no two are parallel, and no three intersect at a single point). It gives the **number of bounded regions** formed, which corresponds directly to the **number of closed shapes** in the figure.
