#### **Key Concept: Bounded Regions Formed by Intersecting Loops**

When **n closed loops** intersect such that **each pair intersects at two points**, the number of **bounded regions** (closed shapes) formed can be computed using the following known geometric result:

$$
\text{Number of bounded regions} = n(n - 1) + 1
$$

This formula applies when:
- Each loop is a **closed curve**.
- Each pair of loops intersects **at two points**.
- **No three loops intersect at the same point**.

This formula is derived from **Euler’s formula** for planar graphs and is also consistent with the known number of regions formed by **intersecting circles**.
