### Step 1: Understand the Geometry

A **3×2×1** rectangular prism is a solid with:

- **Length = 3 units**
- **Width = 2 units**
- **Height = 1 unit**

This configuration implies the following:

- **Front and back faces**: Each is a rectangle of size $3 \times 1$, so each has an area of 3 square units.
- **Left and right faces**: Each is a rectangle of size $2 \times 1$, so each has an area of 2 square units.
- **Top and bottom faces**: Each is a rectangle of size $3 \times 2$, so each has an area of 6 square units.

---

### Step 2: Calculate the Total Surface Area

The surface area of a rectangular prism is given by the formula:

$$
\text{Surface Area} = 2(lw + lh + wh)
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
\text{Surface Area} = 2(3 \times 2 + 3 \times 1 + 2 \times 1) = 2(6 + 3 + 2) = 2 \times 11 = 22
$$

### Step 3: Verification via Unit Cube Count

Each of the 6 unit cubes has 6 faces, so the total number of faces (before accounting for adjacency) is:

$$
6 \times 6 = 36
$$

However, when cubes are adjacent, the shared faces are **not** part of the external surface area. In a 3×2×1 block, the number of adjacent pairs (where two cubes share a face) is:

- **Horizontal (length)**: 2 adjacencies per row × 2 rows = 4
- **Vertical (width)**: 1 adjacency per column × 3 columns = 3

Total adjacent pairs = $4 + 3 = 7$

$$
