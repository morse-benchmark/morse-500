To determine the **surface area** of the 3D structure shown in the video, we analyze it as a composite of unit cubes (each with side length 1 unit). The structure consists of **5 unit cubes**, arranged in a specific configuration:

- A **2x2 base** on the bottom layer (z = 1), consisting of cubes at positions:
  - (1,1,1)
  - (1,2,1)
  - (2,1,1)
  - (2,2,1)

- One **additional cube** stacked on top of (2,2,1), at position:
  - (2,2,2)

---

### Step 1: Total Faces Without Adjacency

Each cube has **6 faces**, and there are **5 cubes**.

$$
\text{Total faces} = 5 \times 6 = 30
$$

---

### Step 2: Count Shared Faces

When two cubes are adjacent (i.e., share a face), that face is **not exposed**. We count how many such shared faces exist between the cubes:

- (1,1,1) and (1,2,1): share a face → 1
- (1,1,1) and (2,1,1): share a face → 1
- (1,2,1) and (2,2,1): share a face → 1
- (2,1,1) and (2,2,1): share a face → 1
- (2,2,1) and (2,2,2): share a face → 1

$$
\text{Total shared faces} = 5
$$

Each shared face hides **2 faces** (one from each cube), so the number of **hidden faces** is:

$$
\text{Hidden faces} = 5 \times 2 = 10
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate Exposed Surface Area

$$
\text{Surface Area} = \text{Total faces} - \text{Hidden faces} = 30 - 10 = 20
$$
