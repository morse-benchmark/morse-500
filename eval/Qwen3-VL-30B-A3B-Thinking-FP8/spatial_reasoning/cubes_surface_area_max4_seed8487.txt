To determine the **surface area** of the 3D structure composed of small unit cubes (each with side length 1), we follow a systematic approach based on the **shared face method**, which is a standard technique for calculating surface area in polycube structures.

---

### ✅ Step 1: Count the Number of Cubes

From analyzing the visual structure (as shown in the video), we count the total number of unit cubes. By examining the structure from multiple angles and identifying all visible and hidden cubes, we find that:

- The structure consists of **9 unit cubes**.

---

### ✅ Step 2: Calculate Total Face Area Without Any Adjacency

Each cube has **6 faces**, so the total number of faces across all 9 cubes is:

$$
9 \times 6 = 54 \text{ faces}
$$

---

### ✅ Step 3: Identify Shared Faces Between Cubes

Each time two cubes share a face (i.e., they are adjacent), that face is **not exposed** and should be subtracted from the total surface area. Since each shared face hides **2** unit faces (one from each cube), we need to count how many **shared faces** exist between adjacent cubes.

We analyze adjacency in three directions:

- **X-direction (left-right)**:  
  - (1,1,1)-(2,1,1)  
  - (2,1,1)-(3,1,1)  
  - (1,2,1)-(2,2,1)  
  - (2,1,2)-(3,1,2)  
  - (2,2,2)-(3,2,2)  
  → **5 shared faces**

- **Y-direction (front-back)**:  
  - (1,1,1)-(1,2,1)  
  - (2,1,1)-(2,2,1)  
  - (2,1,2)-(2,2,2)  
  - (3,1,2)-(3,2,2)  
  → **4 shared faces**

- **Z-direction (top-bottom)**:  
  - (2,1,1)-(2,1,2)  
  - (3,1,1)-(3,1,2)  
  - (2,2,1)-(2,2,2)  
  → **3 shared faces**

**Total shared faces = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12**

---

### ✅ Step 4: Compute the Surface Area

Each shared face hides **2** unit faces (one from each cube). Therefore, the total number of **hidden faces** is:

$$
12 \times 2 = 24
$$

Subtracting these hidden faces from the total face count gives the **exposed surface area**:

$$
\text{Surface Area} = 54 - 24 = \boxed{30}
$$
