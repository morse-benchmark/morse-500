We are told to remember the color names associated with shapes:

- Blue → blue square
- Red → red square
- Orange → orange square
- Green → green square
- Yellow → yellow square
- Purple → purple square
- White → white square

Then, we are instructed to observe the trajectory of an arrow.

In the video, we see:

- A white circle (white shape) appears on the left.
- A yellow circle (yellow shape) appears at the bottom.
- An orange triangle (orange shape) appears on the right.

Then, an arrow is drawn:

1. From the **white circle** (left) → it curves upward and then down toward the **orange triangle** (right). This is a long, curved path.

2. Then, from the **orange triangle** → a short, direct arrow goes to the **yellow circle** (bottom). This is a short, straight path.

The question is: **Which color shape took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape?**

Assuming the arrow’s movement speed is constant, the **shortest path = shortest time**.

- White → Orange: long curved path.
- Orange → Yellow: short straight path.

Therefore, the **yellow** shape took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape (orange).

Answer: **yellow**
