We are shown a scene with several colored lines (orange, white, red, purple) and a dotted line that crosses through them. The question is: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the question

We are not being asked how many times the dotted line intersects the colored lines. Instead, we are being asked how many **sections** (i.e., segments) the colored lines are divided into **by the dotted line**.

That is, every time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it potentially splits that colored line into more sections.

---

### Step 2: Recall the rule

For a single continuous line (like a curve or straight line), if a transversal (here, the dotted line) intersects it **n times**, then it divides that line into **n + 1 sections**.

But here, we have **multiple colored lines**, and we need the **total number of sections** that **all** colored lines are cut into by the dotted line.

So we need to:

1. Count how many times the dotted line intersects **each** colored line.
2. For each colored line, add 1 to the number of intersections to get the number of sections it is divided into.
3. Sum over all colored lines.

---

### Step 3: Identify the colored lines

From the video (frames 0:05–0:10), we can see:

- **Orange line** (thick, curved)
- **White line** (thick, wavy)
- **Red line** (thin, curved, appears behind white and orange)
- **Purple line** (dashed, thin, inside the white curve)

So, **four distinct colored lines**.

---

### Step 4: Count intersections per line with the dotted line

We must carefully observe how many times the **dotted line** crosses each colored line.

Let’s go one by one.

#### 1. Orange line

The dotted line crosses the orange line **once** — near the top left.

→ Sections: 1 + 1 = **2**

#### 2. White line

The dotted line crosses the white line **twice** — once near the bottom left, once near the middle.

→ Sections: 2 + 1 = **3**

#### 3. Red line

The dotted line crosses the red line **once** — near the middle right.

→ Sections: 1 + 1 = **2**

#### 4. Purple line

The dotted line crosses the purple line **once** — near the bottom left, inside the white curve.

→ Sections: 1 + 1 = **2**

---

### Step 5: Total sections

Add them up:

- Orange: 2
- White: 3
- Red: 2
- Purple: 2

**Total = 2 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 9**

---

### Final Answer:

$\boxed{9}$
