### Step 2: Surface area of a single cube

Each cube has 6 faces. If each face is 1x1, then area per face = 1 unit².

So, 8 separate cubes would have total surface area = 8 × 6 = **48 unit²**.

But cubes are connected — when two cubes are adjacent, they share a face, and that face is **not** part of the external surface area.

So we must **subtract 2 units² for every shared face** (since each shared face removes one face from each of two cubes).

- Along the x-direction (left-right): In each layer (there are 2 layers in z), and 2 rows in y, there are 1 shared face per row → 2 layers × 2 rows × 1 = **4** shared faces.
- Along the y-direction (front-back): In each layer (2 layers in z), and 2 columns in x, there are 1 shared face per column → 2 × 2 × 1 = **4**.
- Along the z-direction (top-bottom): Between the two layers, each of the 4 positions has a shared face → **4**.

So total surface area = 48 - (12 × 2) = 48 - 24 = **24**.
