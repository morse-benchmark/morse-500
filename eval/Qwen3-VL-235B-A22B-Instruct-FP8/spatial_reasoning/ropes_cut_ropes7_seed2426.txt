We are asked: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

### Step 1: Understand the question

We are not being asked how many times the dotted line intersects the colored lines. We are being asked: **how many *sections* (i.e., pieces) are created in the *colored lines* as a result of being cut by the dotted line**.

Each time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it *splits* that line into more sections. If a line is crossed *n* times by the dotted line, it is divided into **n + 1** sections.

So, we must:

1. Count how many times the dotted line intersects each colored line.
2. For each colored line, add 1 to the number of intersections to get the number of sections it is divided into.
3. Sum all those sections across all colored lines.
