We are asked to determine the color of the 4th shape visited by the arrow in the trajectory shown.

First, recall the color associations from the beginning of the video:

- Blue → light blue square
- Red → red square
- Orange → orange square
- Green → green square
- Yellow → yellow triangle
- Purple → purple circle
- White → white square

Now, observe the trajectory of the arrow:

1. The arrow starts at the **green circle** (bottom left). This is the **1st** shape visited.
2. It then moves to the **yellow triangle** (top left). This is the **2nd** shape visited.
3. Next, it moves to the **red triangle** (center). This is the **3rd** shape visited.
4. Then, it moves to the **white square** (bottom right). This is the **4th** shape visited.

Therefore, the 4th shape visited is the **white square**.

The color of the 4th shape is: **white**
