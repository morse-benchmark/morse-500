We are shown a 3D structure made of colored cubes, rotating slowly. The structure appears to be a 2x2x2 cube with one or more cubes missing — specifically, it looks like a corner cube is missing, leaving a concave shape.

The question asks: “Which shape matches the missing cubes?” — meaning, which of the options A, B, C, D, or E (None) shows the shape that, when added, would complete the structure into a full 2x2x2 cube?

Let’s analyze the structure:

- The visible structure has 7 cubes (since a full 2x2x2 cube has 8).
- The missing cube is at one corner — specifically, from the rotating views, we can see that the “top-front-right” (or similar) corner is missing.
- The missing piece is a single cube — not a complex shape.

Now look at the options:

- **Option A**: Shows a single cube (red) attached to a green cube — that’s two cubes. Too many.
- **Option B**: Shows a single red cube — this could be the missing piece.
- **Option C**: Shows a 2x2x1 flat layer — 4 cubes. Too many.
- **Option D**: Shows a 2x2x2 cube — that’s the whole thing, not the missing part.
- **Option E**: None of the above.

But wait — Option B is a single red cube. That matches the size and shape of the missing piece.

However, we must check the *orientation and color placement*.

In the rotating structure, the missing cube’s position is adjacent to:
- A green cube (on the left or back),
- A yellow cube (below or front),
- A blue cube (on the side).

The missing cube should have:
- A red face visible (if we assume the red face is on the outer surface of the missing position).

Looking at Option B: it shows a single red cube. But is that accurate?

Actually, in the original structure, the cube that is missing is not just any cube — it’s a cube that would have red on one face, but also connect to green, yellow, and blue cubes.

But since the missing piece is a single cube, and Option B is a single cube, and the color red is visible in the structure adjacent to the gap, it’s reasonable to assume that the missing cube has a red face.

Moreover, none of the other options are single cubes. Option A has two cubes, C has four, D has eight.

So the only option that is a single cube is **B**.

Therefore, **B** is the correct answer.
