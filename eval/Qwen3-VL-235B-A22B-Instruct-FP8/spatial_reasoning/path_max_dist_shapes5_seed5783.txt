We are given a sequence of shapes moving along a path, and we need to determine which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path.

First, let’s recall the color-to-shape mapping from the beginning:

- Blue → square (but in the animation, blue is a circle)
- Red → circle (but in animation, red is not used)
- Orange → triangle (but in animation, orange is a circle)
- Green → square (but in animation, green is a triangle)
- Yellow → square (in animation, yellow is a square)
- Purple → circle (in animation, purple is a circle)
- White → circle (in animation, white is a circle)

Actually, let’s ignore the initial mapping because in the animation, the shapes are shown with specific colors and forms:

At 0:11, we see:
- Yellow square (top left)
- Green triangle (top center)
- Blue circle (bottom left)
- Purple circle (bottom center)
- Orange circle (right)

Then the arrow trajectory begins:

1. Arrow starts at the blue circle (bottom left) → moves to the purple circle (bottom center).  
   → Distance: short, adjacent horizontally.

2. From purple circle → moves to orange circle (right).  
   → Distance: longer than previous, moves rightward.

3. From orange circle → moves leftward to the yellow square (top left).  
   → This is a long diagonal move across the screen.

4. From yellow square → moves to green triangle (top center).  
   → Short move to the right.

Now, the question is: **Which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path?**

We need to compare the distances between consecutive shapes in the path:

- Blue → Purple: short
- Purple → Orange: medium
- Orange → Yellow: **longest** (crosses from right to top-left)
- Yellow → Green: short

So the largest gap is between **Orange** and **Yellow**.

The question asks: “Which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path?”

That is, which shape (by color) had the greatest distance from the one before it?

The shape that came after the long jump is **Yellow** (the square).

So, the **Yellow** shape was the furthest from the previous shape (Orange).

Therefore, the answer is:

$\boxed{yellow}$
