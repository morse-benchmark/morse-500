### Step 1: Understand what a “closed shape” means here.

In this context, a “closed shape” is a region bounded entirely by line segments (or curves) from the drawn lines, such that it forms a distinct, enclosed area — like a polygon or a curved region with no openings.

This is similar to counting the number of “faces” or “regions” created by intersecting lines in a plane, **excluding the infinite outer region**.

However, note: the question says “closed shapes”, which typically implies **bounded** regions — i.e., not the outer unbounded area.
