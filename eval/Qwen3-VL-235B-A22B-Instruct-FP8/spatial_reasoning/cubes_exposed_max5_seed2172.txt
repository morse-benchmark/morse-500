From the video, the structure appears to be a 3x3x3 cube with some cubes removed — specifically, it looks like the center cube of each face and the very center cube of the entire 3x3x3 are missing. This is a common puzzle structure.

- A full 3x3x3 cube has 27 small cubes.
- In the video, we can see that the structure is hollowed out — there are gaps in the center of each face and in the very center.

This is the classic “3x3x3 cube with center and face centers removed” structure.

Total removed = 7 cubes.

So, total cubes present = 27 - 7 = **20 cubes**.

### Step 2: Classify cubes by number of exposed faces

In a 3x3x3 cube, cubes can have:

- **3 faces exposed**: corner cubes (8 total in full cube)

- **1 face exposed**: face-center cubes — 6 total in full cube
- **0 faces exposed**: the very center cube — 1 total

- The 6 face-center cubes → these would have had 1 face exposed
- The 1 center cube → had 0 faces exposed

So, in the **remaining structure**, we have:

- All 8 corner cubes → each has 3 faces exposed → **not what we want**
- All 12 edge cubes → each has 2 faces exposed → **not what we want**
- The 6 face-center cubes → **removed**, so none left
- The center cube → **removed**
