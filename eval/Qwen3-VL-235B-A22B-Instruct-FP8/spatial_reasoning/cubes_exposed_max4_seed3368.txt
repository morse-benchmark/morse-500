### Step 1: Understand what "exposed faces" means
A cube has 6 faces. A face is "exposed" if it is not touching another cube (or is not internal to the structure). So, if a cube is surrounded on 2 sides, then 4 faces are exposed.

We are looking for cubes that have **exactly 4 exposed faces**, meaning they are touching **exactly 2 other cubes** (since 6 - 2 = 4).
