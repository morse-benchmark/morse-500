The question is: **Which color shape was the closest to the previous shape in the path?**

We need to interpret “closest to the previous shape in the path” — meaning, as the arrow moves from one shape to the next, which *pair* of consecutive shapes in the path were physically closest to each other?

1. **Start at yellow circle (bottom center) → moves to red square (top right)**  
   - This is a long diagonal movement.

2. **Red square (top right) → moves to purple square (top left)**  
   - This is a horizontal movement across the top. The red and purple squares are both near the top edge, so this distance is relatively short.

3. **Purple square (top left) → returns to yellow circle (bottom center)**  
   - Another long diagonal movement.

So, the shortest segment in the path is between the **red square** and the **purple square**.

Therefore, the shape that was closest to the previous shape in the path is the **purple** shape (since it followed the red shape, and they were closest to each other).

But note: the question says “Which color shape was the closest to the previous shape in the path?”

This could be interpreted as: at the point in the path where two consecutive shapes were closest, what was the color of the *current* shape (i.e., the one being approached)?

In that case, when moving from red to purple, the “current” shape is **purple**, and it is closest to the previous shape (red).

Alternatively, if the question is asking for the color of the shape that was closest to its predecessor, then again, **purple** is the answer.

Let’s confirm visually:  
- Yellow to red: long diagonal
