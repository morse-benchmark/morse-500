We are shown a 3D structure made of small colored cubes, rotating on screen. The question asks: **“How many cubes have exactly 1 face exposed?”**

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

From the rotating views (frames 0:04–0:10), we can deduce the structure is made of **8 small cubes** arranged in a 2x2x2 block, **but with one cube missing** — specifically, the front-top-right cube is absent. So it’s a 2x2x2 cube with one corner cube removed.

This leaves **7 cubes** in total.

---

### Step 2: Define “exposed face”

An “exposed face” means a face of a small cube that is visible from the outside — i.e., not touching another cube or hidden inside.

We are to count how many **cubes** have **exactly one** such face exposed.

---

### Step 3: Analyze each cube’s position

Let’s label the positions in the 2x2x2 cube as:

- Front-Top-Left (FTL)
- Front-Top-Right (FTR) → **missing**
- Front-Bottom-Left (FBL)
- Front-Bottom-Right (FBR)
- Back-Top-Left (BTL)
- Back-Top-Right (BTR)
- Back-Bottom-Left (BBL)
- Back-Bottom-Right (BBR)

We remove FTR.

Now, for each remaining cube, count how many of its 6 faces are exposed (i.e., not adjacent to another cube).

---

#### 1. FTL (Front-Top-Left)

Adjacent to:
- Right: FTR → **missing** → so right face exposed
- Bottom: FBL → present → covered
- Back: BTL → present → covered

Also, front, top, left faces are on the exterior.

So exposed faces: **front, top, left, right** → 4 exposed faces.

→ Not 1.

---

#### 2. FBL (Front-Bottom-Left)

Adjacent to:
- Top: FTL → present → covered
- Right: FBR → present → covered
- Back: BBL → present → covered

Exposed faces: **front, bottom, left** → 3 exposed.

→ Not 1.

---

#### 3. FBR (Front-Bottom-Right)

Adjacent to:
- Left: FBL → present → covered
- Top: FTR → **missing** → top face exposed
- Back: BBR → present → covered

Exposed faces: **front, bottom, right, top** → 4 exposed.

→ Not 1.

---

#### 4. BTL (Back-Top-Left)

Adjacent to:
- Front: FTL → present → covered

#### 5. BTR (Back-Top-Right)

- Bottom: BBR → present → covered

Exposed faces: **back, top, right, front** → 4 exposed.

→ Not 1.

---

#### 6. BBL (Back-Bottom-Left)

Adjacent to:
- Front: FBL → present → covered
- Right: BBR → present → covered
- Top: BTL → present → covered

Exposed faces: **back, bottom, left** → 3 exposed.

#### 7. BBR (Back-Bottom-Right)

→ Not 1.

---

Wait — **none** of the cubes have exactly 1 face exposed?

That can’t be right. Let’s reconsider.

Perhaps I miscounted. Maybe one cube is **completely surrounded** except for one face?

In a 2x2x2 with one corner missing, is there any cube that has 5 faces covered?

No — because every cube is on the corner or edge.

Actually, let’s think differently.

In a full 2x2x2 cube, every cube has 3 faces exposed (since each is a corner cube).

When we remove one cube, the cubes adjacent to the missing cube gain **one additional exposed face**.

So:

- The 3 cubes that were adjacent to the missing cube (FTL, FBR, BTR) now have **4 exposed faces** each.
- The other 4 cubes (FBL, BTL, BBL, BBR) still have **3 exposed faces** each.

So all 7 cubes have **3 or 4 exposed faces**.

→ **None have exactly 1 exposed face.**

But the question asks “how many cubes have exactly 1 face exposed?” — and expects a single integer.

So the answer must be **0**.

---

### Final Answer:

$\boxed{0}$
