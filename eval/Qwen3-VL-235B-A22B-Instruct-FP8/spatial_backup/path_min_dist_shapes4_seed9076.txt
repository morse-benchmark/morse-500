Then we are shown a trajectory of an arrow moving between colored dots:

1. The arrow starts at a **yellow** dot (top center).
2. It moves in an arc to a **red** dot (slightly right of yellow).
3. Then it moves down and right to a **blue** dot (bottom right).
4. Then it moves up and left to a **green** dot (bottom left).

The question is: **Which color shape was the closest to the previous shape in the path?**

We need to evaluate the spatial proximity between consecutive shapes along the arrow’s path.

Let’s list the path sequence:

- Step 1: Yellow → Red
- Step 2: Red → Blue
- Step 3: Blue → Green

We are to find **which color shape** (i.e., which *current* shape in the sequence) was **closest to the previous shape**.

So we compare:

- Distance from Yellow to Red → very small (they are adjacent horizontally)
- Distance from Red to Blue → larger, diagonal down-right
- Distance from Blue to Green → also large, diagonal up-left

Visually, the **Red** dot is closest to the **Yellow** dot — they are nearly touching, while the others are much farther apart.

Therefore, the shape that was closest to the previous shape in the path is **Red** (since Red followed Yellow, and was very close to it).

✅ Final Answer: **red**
