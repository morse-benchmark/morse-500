We are given a sequence of colored shapes and an arrow that traces a path between them. The task is to determine which color shape was the *furthest from the previous shape in the path*.

---

### Step 1: Recall the color names from the initial screen

From 0:03, we are shown:

- Blue (square)
- Red (square)
- Orange (square)
- Green (square)
- Yellow (square)
- Purple (square)
- White (square)

Each color is associated with a square shape.

---

### Step 2: Observe the trajectory of the arrow

At 0:11, we see four colored shapes on screen:

- Red square (bottom left)
- Purple circle (top right)
- Yellow circle (middle right)
- Blue circle (bottom right)

Then, at 0:13, an arrow starts at the red square and moves toward the blue circle.

At 0:15, the arrow branches: one path goes from blue to yellow, and another from blue to purple.

At 0:16, the arrow continues from yellow to purple.

At 0:17, the arrow goes from purple back toward the red square.

At 0:18, the arrow reaches the red square.

So the full path traced by the arrow is:

**Red → Blue → Yellow → Purple → Red**

---

### Step 3: Determine the distances between consecutive shapes in the path

We need to visually estimate which segment had the *greatest distance* between two consecutive shapes.

Let’s analyze each segment:

1. **Red → Blue**: Red is bottom left, Blue is bottom right. Horizontal distance — moderate.

2. **Blue → Yellow**: Blue is bottom right, Yellow is middle right. Vertical distance — short.

3. **Yellow → Purple**: Yellow is middle right, Purple is top right. Vertical distance — moderate, but shorter than Red→Blue.

4. **Purple → Red**: Purple is top right, Red is bottom left. This is a diagonal across the screen — clearly the *longest distance*.

---

### Step 4: Identify the color shape that was furthest from the *previous* shape

The question asks: “Which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path?”

That means: for each shape in the path (except the first), we compare its distance from the one before it.

- Blue was after Red → distance: moderate
- Yellow was after Blue → distance: short
- Purple was after Yellow → distance: moderate
- **Red was after Purple → distance: longest**

So the shape that was furthest from the previous one is **Red** (since it came after Purple, and the distance from Purple to Red is the greatest).

---

### Final Answer:

$\boxed{\text{red}}$
