### Step 1: Understand what "exactly 4 faces exposed" means.

Each small cube has 6 faces. In a 3D structure, some faces are hidden because they are adjacent to other cubes or are on the interior. A cube with **exactly 4 faces exposed** must be touching **exactly 2 other cubes** (since each adjacent cube hides one face).

Alternatively, we can think of it as: a cube has 4 faces visible → it must be connected to 2 other cubes (6 - 2 = 4 exposed).
