We are shown a scene with multiple intersecting lines of different colors, and then asked:

> “How many closed shapes do the intersecting lines create? Answer with a single integer.”

---

### Step 1: Understand what a “closed shape” means in this context.

A **closed shape** is a region completely enclosed by line segments (or curves) such that there is no way to exit the region without crossing a line. In other words, it’s a bounded area formed by the intersections of the lines.

This is similar to counting **faces** or **regions** in a planar graph formed by intersecting curves.

---

- The goal is to count **all** enclosed regions — not just the obvious large ones, but **every** bounded area formed by the intersections.

This is not trivial by eye, because:

- Some regions are very small.
- Some are nested.
- Some are formed by multiple lines intersecting.
