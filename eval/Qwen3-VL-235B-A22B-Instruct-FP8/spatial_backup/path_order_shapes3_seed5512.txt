We are shown a sequence of colored squares and an arrow that traces a trajectory, visiting them in order.

First, let’s recall the color associations from the beginning of the video:

- Light blue square → blue
- Red square → red
- Orange square → orange
- Green square → green
- Yellow square → yellow
- Purple square → purple
- White square → white

Then, at 0:10, we see the setup: three squares appear:

- A red square (left side)
- An orange square (bottom center)
- A green square (top right)

At 0:12, an arrow appears and begins its trajectory.

Let’s trace the arrow’s path step by step:

1. **First, the arrow starts near the red square and moves toward it.**  
   → The arrow points to the red square first.  
   → So, the **1st shape visited is red**.

2. **Then, the arrow curves downward and moves to the orange square.**  
   → The arrow points to the orange square next.  
   → So, the **2nd shape visited is orange**.

3. **Finally, the arrow curves upward and moves to the green square.**  
   → The arrow points to the green square last.  
   → So, the **3rd shape visited is green**.

The question asks:  
> What was the color of the 2nd shape to be visited?

From our tracing, the 2nd shape visited is the **orange** square.

Therefore, the answer is:

$\boxed{\text{orange}}$
