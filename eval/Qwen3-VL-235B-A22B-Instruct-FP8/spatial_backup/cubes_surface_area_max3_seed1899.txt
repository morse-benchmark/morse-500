We are shown a 3D structure made of small cubes, and we are to find its **surface area**, assuming each small cube has side length 1 unit.

### Step 1: Understand the structure

From the rotating 3D model (frames 0:04–0:10), we can observe that the structure is made of **6 small cubes**.

It appears to be a 2x2x2 cube with **two cubes missing** — specifically, the top-front-right and top-back-left cubes (or similar configuration). But let’s verify by counting visible faces and reasoning carefully.

Alternatively, we can count the number of **exposed faces**.

Each cube has 6 faces. If cubes are glued together, the glued faces are **not** part of the surface area.

So, total surface area = (Total faces of all cubes) - 2 × (number of glued faces)

### Step 3: Count the number of glued (shared) faces

We need to count how many pairs of cubes are adjacent (i.e., sharing a face).

Let’s try to reconstruct the structure.

From the rotating views, it looks like:

- A base layer of 4 cubes in a 2x2 square.
- On top of that, only 2 cubes: one on the front-left and one on the back-right (diagonally opposite).

So the structure is:

- Bottom layer: 4 cubes (positions: front-left, front-right, back-left, back-right)
- Top layer: 2 cubes (on front-left and back-right)

Now, count shared faces:

#### Within bottom layer:

- Each cube in the 2x2 layer shares faces with its neighbors.

In a 2x2 square:

- Each internal edge is shared by 2 cubes.

There are:

- 2 horizontal shared faces (between front-left/front-right and back-left/back-right)
- 2 vertical shared faces (between front-left/back-left and front-right/back-right)

→ **4 shared faces** in bottom layer.

#### Between bottom and top:

- Top-front-left cube sits on bottom-front-left → 1 shared face
- Top-back-right cube sits on bottom-back-right → 1 shared face

→ **2 shared faces**

#### Within top layer:

The two top cubes are **not adjacent** — they are diagonally opposite, so they don’t share a face.

→ **0 shared faces**

Total shared (glued) faces = 4 (bottom) + 2 (between layers) = **6**

Each shared face means 2 faces are hidden (one from each cube), so total hidden faces = 6 × 2 = **12**

### Step 4: Compute surface area

Total faces if separate: 6 cubes × 6 = 36

Minus hidden faces: 36 - 12 = **24**
