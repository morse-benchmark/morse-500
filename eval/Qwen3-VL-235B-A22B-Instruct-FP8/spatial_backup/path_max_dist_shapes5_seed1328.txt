(Note: The video shows colored shapes, and we must associate the color with the shape’s color, not the shape type necessarily — but since the question asks for “color shape”, we interpret it as the color of the shape.)

Now, let’s trace the arrow’s trajectory step by step:

1. **Starts at red triangle** (top right) → moves to **blue triangle** (center) → then to **purple triangle** (top left) → then to **yellow square** (bottom left) → then to **orange square** (bottom right).

So the sequence of shapes visited is:

- Red triangle
- Blue triangle
- Purple triangle
- Yellow square
- Orange square

Now, we need to find which step had the **greatest distance** between consecutive shapes.

Let’s estimate the relative distances visually:

- **Red → Blue**: from top right to center — moderate distance.
- **Blue → Purple**: from center to top left — similar distance, maybe slightly longer.
- **Purple → Yellow**: from top left to bottom left — vertical drop, likely longer than previous.
- **Yellow → Orange**: from bottom left to bottom right — horizontal across the bottom — this looks like the **longest** distance visually.

Comparing the last two:

- Purple to Yellow: vertical movement, but not full screen height.
- Yellow to Orange: horizontal movement from left to right — appears to span almost the full width of the screen.

In most such visual tests, horizontal or diagonal spans across the screen are longer than vertical or shorter diagonal moves.

Thus, the **greatest distance** is between **Yellow square** and **Orange square**.

The question asks: **Which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path?**

That is, which shape (by color) was the one that was furthest *from the one before it*.

So, the shape that was furthest from its predecessor is the **Orange square**.
