### Step 4: Standard method for surface area of a polycube

For a structure made of unit cubes, the surface area is:

> Total surface area = 6 * (number of cubes) - 2 * (number of shared faces)

Each cube has 6 faces. When two cubes are adjacent, they share a face, and that face is not part of the external surface area. So we subtract 2 for each shared face (one from each cube).

So, if we can count the number of cubes and the number of shared faces, we can compute the surface area.
