We are shown a scene with multiple colored lines (curves and straight lines) and a single dotted line that cuts across them. The question is: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the question

We are not being asked how many times the dotted line intersects the colored lines — that would be the number of intersection *points*.

Instead, we are being asked how many *sections* (i.e., segments) the colored lines are divided into *by the dotted line*.

Each time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it cuts that line into more pieces. For a single continuous line, if it is intersected *n* times by another line, it is divided into **n + 1** sections.

But here, we have **multiple colored lines**, each possibly intersected multiple times by the dotted line.

So, we need to:

1. Count how many times the dotted line intersects **each** colored line.
2. For each colored line, the number of sections it is cut into = (number of intersections) + 1.
3. Sum this over all colored lines.

---

### Step 2: Identify and count the colored lines

From the video (frames 0:04–0:10), we can see the following colored lines:

- Yellow (thick, wavy)
- Red (curved)
- Purple (dashed, curved)
- Blue (curved)
- Orange (curved)
- Green (curved, added later)
- White (straight, added later)

That’s **7 distinct colored lines**.

Note: The white line is added at 0:06, and the green at 0:05 — they are part of the scene when the dotted line appears.

---

### Step 3: Count intersections per line with the dotted line

We must now count how many times the **dotted line** intersects **each** of these 7 lines.

Let’s go one by one, visually inspecting the frame at 0:07 (when all lines are present and the dotted line is fully visible).

#### 1. Yellow line:
- The dotted line crosses the yellow line **twice**.
→ Sections = 2 + 1 = **3**

#### 2. Red line:
- Crosses **twice**.
→ Sections = 2 + 1 = **3**

#### 3. Purple line (dashed):
- Crosses **once**.
→ Sections = 1 + 1 = **2**

#### 4. Blue line:
- Crosses **twice**.
→ Sections = 2 + 1 = **3**

#### 5. Orange line:
- Crosses **once**.
→ Sections = 1 + 1 = **2**

#### 6. Green line:
- Crosses **once**.
→ Sections = 1 + 1 = **2**

#### 7. White line:
- Crosses **once**.
→ Sections = 1 + 1 = **2**

---

### Step 4: Sum all sections

Now add them up:

- Yellow: 3
- Red: 3
- Purple: 2
- Blue: 3
- Orange: 2
- Green: 2
- White: 2

Total = 3 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 = **17**
