We are shown a 3D structure made of colored cubes (red, blue, green, yellow) that rotates slowly, allowing us to see it from multiple angles. Then, we are asked to match it to one of the four options (A, B, C, D) or choose “E. None of the above.”

---

### Step 1: Analyze the original structure

From the rotating view (0:03–0:10), we can observe:

- The structure is made of 10 cubes (counted by visible and inferred positions).
- It has a central “core” with cubes extending in multiple directions.
- The color pattern is consistent:
  - There is a red cube on the left side (visible at 0:04, 0:06, 0:08).
  - A yellow cube is on the bottom front (visible at 0:05, 0:07, 0:09).
  - A green cube is on the top right (visible at 0:04, 0:06, 0:08).
  - Blue cubes form much of the central and back structure.

The structure is not symmetrical, so orientation matters.

---

### Step 2: Compare with options A, B, C, D

We need to find which option is a **rotation** (not a reflection or different structure) of the original.

Let’s examine each:

#### Option A:
- Looks very similar to the original.
- Red cube on the left, yellow on bottom front, green on top right — matches the original’s color placement.
- The overall shape and relative positions of cubes appear identical.
- It may be a slightly rotated version (perhaps rotated around the vertical axis), but the spatial relationships are preserved.

#### Option B:
- The red cube is now on the **bottom**, not the side.
- The yellow cube is on the **top**, not the bottom front.
- This suggests a different orientation or possibly a reflection — but let’s check if it’s a valid rotation.

Actually, rotating the original 90° forward might bring the red cube to the bottom, but then the yellow cube would not be on top — it would be on the back or side. So this does not match.

#### Option C:
- Red cube is on the **front right**, not the left.
- Yellow cube is on the **top left**.
- This is inconsistent with the original’s color positions — likely a reflection or different structure.

#### Option D:
- Red cube is on the **bottom right**.
- Yellow cube is on the **top left**.
- Again, color positions don’t match the original.

---

### Step 3: Determine the correct match

Only **Option A** preserves the relative positions and colors as seen in the original structure from different angles. The orientation is slightly different (rotated), but that’s expected — the question is asking for a matching shape, not identical orientation.

The structure in Option A is a valid rotation of the original.

---

### Final Answer:

$\boxed{\text{A}}$
