We are asked: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the scene

From the video frames:

- There are several **colored lines** (orange, white, purple, red) drawn on the screen. These are continuous curves or lines, some intersecting each other.
- A **dotted line** is drawn diagonally across the scene, from bottom-left to top-right.
- The dotted line **intersects** some of the colored lines.

The question is not asking how many times the dotted line intersects the colored lines, but rather: **how many *sections* (i.e., segments) are created in the colored lines *as a whole* by the dotted line?**

That is, each time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it cuts that line into more pieces. We need to count the **total number of segments** that all the colored lines are divided into by the dotted line.

---

### Step 2: Key principle

For a single continuous line:

- If a line is intersected **n times** by a transversal (the dotted line), it is divided into **n + 1** segments.

So, for multiple colored lines, we need to:

1. Count how many times the dotted line intersects **each** colored line.
2. For each colored line, compute the number of segments it’s divided into: **intersections + 1**.
3. Sum over all colored lines.
