### Step 2: Compute surface area

Each small cube has 6 faces, each of area 1 unit².

If all 20 cubes were isolated, total surface area would be 20 × 6 = 120.

But when cubes are adjacent, they share faces — and those shared faces are **not part of the external surface area**.

So we must subtract **2 for each shared face** (since each shared face is internal and was counted twice — once for each cube).

So, we need to count the number of **adjacent face pairs** (i.e., shared faces between two cubes).
