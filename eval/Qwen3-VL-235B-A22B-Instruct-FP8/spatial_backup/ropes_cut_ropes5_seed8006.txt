We are shown a scene with multiple colored lines (curves) and a vertical dotted line that intersects them. The question asks: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the question

We are to count the **total number of sections** that the **colored lines** are divided into **by the dotted line**.

Each time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it splits that line into more sections.

For a single continuous line:
- 0 crossings → 1 section
- 1 crossing → 2 sections
- 2 crossings → 3 sections
- ...
- **n crossings → n+1 sections**

So, for each colored line, if it is crossed **k times** by the dotted line, it is divided into **k+1 sections**.

We need to:
1. Identify each colored line.
2. Count how many times the dotted line crosses each.
3. For each, add (crossings + 1).
4. Sum all those values.
