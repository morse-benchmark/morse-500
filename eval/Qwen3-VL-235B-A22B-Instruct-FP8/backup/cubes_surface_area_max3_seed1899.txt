### Step 2: Surface area of a single cube

Each cube has 6 faces. If side = 1 unit, then area of each face = 1 unit².

So, 6 cubes × 6 faces = 36 faces.

But — **when cubes are glued together, the faces that are touching are not part of the external surface area**.

So we need to subtract the area of the **hidden faces** (i.e., faces that are glued to another cube).

---

### Step 3: Count the number of glued (hidden) faces

Each time two cubes are adjacent (sharing a face), we lose 2 faces from the total surface area (one from each cube).

So we need to count the number of **adjacent pairs**.

Let’s label the cubes for clarity:

Assume:

- Cube A: center cube.
- Cube B: attached to front of A.
- Cube C: attached to back of A.
- Cube D: attached to left of A.
- Cube E: attached to right of A.
- Cube F: attached to top of A.

So, cube A is connected to B, C, D, E, F → **5 connections**.

Are there any other connections? For example, is B connected to anything else? From the video, no — all other cubes are only connected to the center cube.

So total number of **adjacent pairs** = 5.

Each adjacent pair hides 2 faces (one from each cube).

So total hidden faces = 5 × 2 = **10 faces**.

---

### Step 4: Compute total surface area

Total faces if separate: 6 cubes × 6 = 36 faces.

Minus hidden faces: 36 - 10 = **26 faces**.

Each face is 1 unit² → surface area = **26 unit²**.

### Final Answer:

$\boxed{26}$
