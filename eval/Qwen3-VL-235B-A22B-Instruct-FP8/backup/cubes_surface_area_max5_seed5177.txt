### Step 2: Alternative approach — Count total faces and subtract hidden ones

Each small cube has 6 faces.

If we can count the **number of cubes**, say `n`, then total faces = `6n`.

But when two cubes are adjacent (sharing a face), those two faces are **hidden** (not part of the surface area).

So:

> **Surface Area = 6n - 2 × (number of shared faces)**

So we need to:

1. Count total cubes `n`.
2. Count how many pairs of cubes are adjacent (sharing a face) — call this `s`.
3. Then SA = 6n - 2s.
