We are asked to determine the color of the 4th shape visited by the arrow’s trajectory.

First, recall the color associations from the beginning of the video:

- Blue → light blue square
- Red → red square
- Orange → orange triangle
- Green → green circle
- Yellow → yellow square
- Purple → purple square
- White → white square

Now, observe the trajectory of the arrow:

At 0:13, the arrow starts at the green circle (on the far right) and moves leftward, passing through:

1. **First shape visited**: The green circle (color: green) — this is the starting point.
2. **Second shape visited**: The orange triangle (color: orange) — the arrow passes through it next.
3. **Third shape visited**: The red square (color: red) — the arrow continues left and passes through it.
4. **Fourth shape visited**: The purple square (color: purple) — the arrow then curves up and left to pass through this one.

We can verify this by watching the arrow’s path:

- From green circle → to orange triangle → to red square → to purple square.

Thus, the 4th shape visited is the **purple square**.

The color name is: **purple**
