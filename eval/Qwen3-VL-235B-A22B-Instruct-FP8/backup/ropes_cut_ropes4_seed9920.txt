We are shown a scene with several colored lines (orange, yellow, purple, cyan) that are drawn in a tangled, overlapping fashion. Then, a vertical dotted line is drawn through the scene, intersecting these colored lines.

The question is: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Understand the question**: We are not counting how many times the dotted line crosses the colored lines. We are counting how many *sections* (i.e., pieces) the colored lines are divided into *by the dotted line*.

   - Each time the dotted line crosses a colored line, it cuts that line into more sections.
   - For a single continuous line, if it is crossed *n* times by the dotted line, it is divided into *n+1* sections.
   - But we have **multiple colored lines**, and we need the **total number of sections** all colored lines are cut into.

2. **Identify the colored lines**:
   - There are 4 distinct colored lines: orange, yellow, purple, cyan.
   - Each is a single continuous line (even if it loops or overlaps with others).

3. **Count the number of times the dotted line intersects each colored line**:

   Let’s examine the frame at 0:07 (where the dotted line is fully visible and intersects all lines):

   - **Orange line**: The dotted line crosses it **once**.
     → Divides it into **2 sections**.

   - **Yellow line**: The dotted line crosses it **twice** (once near the top, once near the bottom).
     → Divides it into **3 sections**.

   - **Purple line**: The dotted line crosses it **once** (near the bottom).
     → Divides it into **2 sections**.

   - **Cyan line**: The dotted line crosses it **twice** (once near the middle, once near the bottom).
     → Divides it into **3 sections**.

4. **Add up all the sections**:

   - Orange: 2
   - Yellow: 3
   - Purple: 2
   - Cyan: 3

   Total = 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 = **10**

---

### Final Answer:
$\boxed{10}$
