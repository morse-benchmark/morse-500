We are asked to count how many times the color **green** appeared on the **top** of any cube **after each roll**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the setup

- There are 4 cubes.
- Each cube is rolled (tumbled) multiple times.
- After each roll, we record the color on the **top** face of **each** cube.
- We are to count the **total number of times green appeared on top** across all cubes and all rolls.

---

### Step 2: Analyze the rolls

We are shown a sequence of rolls. Let’s go frame by frame and record the top color of each cube after each roll.

There are 4 cubes. Let’s label them from left to right as Cube 1, Cube 2, Cube 3, Cube 4.

At 0:11, we see the cubes in their initial orientation:

- Cube 1: top = red
- Cube 2: top = yellow
- Cube 3: top = purple
- Cube 4: top = red

→ No green on top.

- Cube 4: top = white

#### Third roll (0:17):

### Step 3: Total green appearances
