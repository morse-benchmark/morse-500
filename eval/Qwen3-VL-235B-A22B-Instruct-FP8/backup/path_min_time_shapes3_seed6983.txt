We are told to remember the color names associated with the shapes:

- Blue → light blue square
- Red → red square
- Orange → orange square
- Green → green square
- Yellow → yellow square
- Purple → purple square
- White → white circle

Then we are asked to observe the trajectory of the arrow.

From 0:11 to 0:16, we see:

1. At 0:11, three shapes appear:
   - Orange square (left)
   - Purple square (top right)
   - White circle (bottom right)

2. At 0:12, an arrow starts from the purple square and moves leftward toward the orange square.

3. At 0:13, the arrow reaches the orange square and then curves downward toward the white circle.

4. At 0:15, the arrow reaches the white circle.

So the sequence of movement is:
**Purple → Orange → White**

The question is: **Which color shape took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape?**

We must compare the time taken for:
- Purple to Orange
- Orange to White

Visually, the arrow moves from purple to orange in a relatively straight, short path (0:12 to 0:13 — about 1 second).

Then from orange to white, the arrow curves and travels a longer path (0:13 to 0:15 — about 2 seconds).

Therefore, the movement from **Purple to Orange** is shorter in time.

The question asks: “Which color shape took the shortest amount of time to reach from the previous shape?”

That is: **Which shape was reached in the shortest time from its predecessor?**

The shape reached in the shortest time is the **orange** shape (reached from purple).

So the answer is: **orange**
