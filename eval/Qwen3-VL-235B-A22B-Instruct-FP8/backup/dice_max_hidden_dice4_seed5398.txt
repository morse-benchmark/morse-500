We are shown a set of four cubes, each with visible faces colored according to a key:

- Blue (light blue square)
- Red (red square)
- White (white square)
- Green (green square)
- Yellow (yellow square)
- Purple (purple square)

The cubes are shown in 3D, so we can see 3 faces of each cube (front, top, and side). That means 3 faces are visible, and 3 are hidden (back, bottom, and the opposite side).

We are to determine: **Out of the faces currently hidden from the camera, which color appears most often?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the setup

Each cube has 6 faces. In the 3D view, we see 3 faces per cube. Therefore, for each cube, 3 faces are hidden.

There are 4 cubes → total hidden faces = 4 × 3 = **12 hidden faces**.

We must deduce the colors of the hidden faces based on the visible ones and the assumption that each cube uses the 6 colors (blue, red, white, green, yellow, purple) — one per face — with no repeats per cube.

This is a standard “cube color deduction” puzzle.

---

### Step 2: Analyze each cube

We’ll go cube by cube, noting the visible colors, and deducing the hidden ones.

#### Cube 1 (leftmost):

Visible faces:
- Front: Yellow
- Top: Blue
- Right side: Green

So visible: yellow, blue, green

Therefore, hidden faces (back, bottom, left side) must be: red, white, purple

→ Hidden colors: red, white, purple

#### Cube 2:

Visible faces:
- Front: Yellow
- Top: Green
- Right side: Blue

Same as Cube 1? Let’s check.

Actually, looking again — in frame 0:11, Cube 2 has:

- Front: Yellow
- Top: Green
- Right: Blue

So same visible colors: yellow, green, blue

→ Hidden: red, white, purple

#### Cube 3:

Visible:
- Front: Blue

- Right: Yellow

So visible: blue, white, yellow

Hidden: red, green, purple

#### Cube 4 (rightmost):

Visible:
- Front: Purple

- Right: Green

So visible: purple, white, green

Hidden: red, blue, yellow

---

### Step 3: List all hidden faces across all 4 cubes

Cube 1 hidden: red, white, purple  
Cube 2 hidden: red, white, purple  
Cube 3 hidden: red, green, purple  
Cube 4 hidden: red, blue, yellow

Now, let’s count frequency of each color among hidden faces:

- red: appears in Cube 1, 2, 3, 4 → **4 times**
- white: Cube 1, 2 → **2 times**
- purple: Cube 1, 2, 3 → **3 times**
- green: Cube 3 → **1 time**
- blue: Cube 4 → **1 time**
- yellow: Cube 4 → **1 time**

---

### Step 4: Determine which color appears most often among hidden faces

Red appears **4 times** — more than any other color.
