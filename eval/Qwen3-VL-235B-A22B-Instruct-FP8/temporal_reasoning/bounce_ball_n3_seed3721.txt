We must answer in seconds, rounded to 1 decimal place.

The video starts at 0.0 seconds with the ball on the left and a single vertical bar on the right.

- At **0.0s**: Ball is on the far left. First wall is on the right.
- At **0.3s**: Ball has moved slightly right.
- At **1.5s**: Ball is closer to the first wall.
- At **2.7s**: Ball hits the first wall (right side) — this is the **first collision**.

But wait — the question is: **“How many seconds did the ball travel before hitting an object?”**

That means: from the start (0.0s) until the **first** time it hits *any* object.

In the video:

- At **2.7s**, the ball hits the **first wall** on the right.

So, the ball traveled for **2.7 seconds** before hitting an object.

But let’s double-check.

At **3.9s**, the ball is bouncing back to the left — meaning it *already hit* the wall before that.

At **5.1s**, we see a second wall appears on the left — but the ball hasn’t hit it yet.

At **6.3s**, the ball hits the *left* wall — but that’s the **second** hit.

So the **first** hit is at **2.7s**.
