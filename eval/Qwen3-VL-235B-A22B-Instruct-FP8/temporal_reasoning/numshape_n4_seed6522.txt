We are given a sequence of objects and numbers appearing in a video. The instruction is:

> **Sum up all the numbers between sphere and rectangular prism. Round to 2 decimal places.**

---

### Step 1: Identify the order of objects and numbers

From the video frames:

1. **Frame 0.2s**: A red sphere appears. → This is the **sphere**.
2. **Frame 1.2s**: The sphere is still visible.
3. **Frame 2.2s**: The sphere disappears, and a **white number "7.45"** appears briefly. → This is the **first number after the sphere**.
4. **Frame 3.2s**: A red cylinder appears. → Not relevant unless it's between sphere and prism.
5. **Frame 4.2s**: The cylinder is still visible, and a **white number "3.14"** appears. → Second number.
6. **Frame 5.2s**: The cylinder disappears, and a **white number "9.87"** appears. → Third number.
7. **Frame 6.2s**: A red rectangular prism appears. → This is the **rectangular prism**.

### Step 2: Determine which numbers are *between* the sphere and the rectangular prism

- The **sphere** appears at 0.2s.
- The **rectangular prism** appears at 6.2s.
- The numbers that appear **after the sphere** and **before the rectangular prism** are:
  - 7.45 (at 2.2s)
  - 3.14 (at 4.2s)
  - 9.87 (at 5.2s)

Note: The cylinder appears at 3.2s, but it’s an object — not a number — so we ignore it for the sum. We only care about the numbers that appear in the time interval between the sphere and the rectangular prism.

7.45 + 3.14 + 9.87 = ?

Let’s compute:

7.45 + 3.14 = 10.59

10.59 + 9.87 = **20.46**

20.46 is already at 2 decimal places.
