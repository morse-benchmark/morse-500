We are asked to:

> **Sum up all the numbers between tetrahedron and octahedron. Round to 2 decimal places.**

---

### Step 1: Understand the context

The video shows various 3D geometric shapes:

- Triangle → likely representing a **tetrahedron** (3D shape with 4 triangular faces)
- Sphere → not a polyhedron, probably a red herring
- Cube → 6 faces
- Diamond-like shape → likely an **octahedron** (8 triangular faces)

The key is the **names** of the shapes: **tetrahedron** and **octahedron**.

In geometry, these are Platonic solids:

- **Tetrahedron** → 4 faces
- **Octahedron** → 8 faces

So, the “numbers” between them likely refer to the **number of faces** of the Platonic solids that come **between** tetrahedron and octahedron in the sequence of Platonic solids ordered by number of faces.

---

### Step 2: List the Platonic solids by number of faces

There are only 5 Platonic solids:

1. **Tetrahedron** — 4 faces
2. **Cube (Hexahedron)** — 6 faces
3. **Octahedron** — 8 faces
4. **Dodecahedron** — 12 faces
5. **Icosahedron** — 20 faces

So, between **tetrahedron (4)** and **octahedron (8)**, the only Platonic solid is the **cube (6)**.

---

### Step 3: Sum up all the numbers between them

The phrase “between tetrahedron and octahedron” likely means **the numbers strictly between 4 and 8**, i.e., 5, 6, 7.

But wait — is it referring to **integers between 4 and 8**, or **Platonic solids between them**?

Let’s consider both interpretations.

---

#### Interpretation A: Integers between 4 and 8 (exclusive)

Numbers: 5, 6, 7

Sum = 5 + 6 + 7 = **18.00**

---

#### Interpretation B: Platonic solids between tetrahedron and octahedron

Only the **cube** (6 faces) lies between them.

Sum = **6.00**

---

#### Interpretation C: Faces of all Platonic solids from tetrahedron to octahedron inclusive?

That would be 4 + 6 + 8 = 18.00 — same as Interpretation A.

But the question says **“between”**, which usually excludes endpoints.

---

### Step 4: Re-examining the video

The video shows:

- Triangle → tetrahedron (4)
- Then sphere (irrelevant?)
- Then cube (6)
- Then diamond → octahedron (8)

So visually, it’s showing **tetrahedron → cube → octahedron**

So the “numbers between” likely refers to the **face counts of the shapes shown between tetrahedron and octahedron**, which is **just the cube (6)**.

But the question says: **“Sum up all the numbers between tetrahedron and octahedron.”**

If we take “numbers” as the **face counts**, and “between” as **in the sequence**, then only 6 is between 4 and 8.

However, if “numbers” means **integers from 5 to 7**, then sum is 18.

But why would the video show geometric shapes if it’s just asking for integers between 4 and 8?

That seems unlikely.

---

### Step 5: Most plausible interpretation

Given the context of geometry and Platonic solids, and the fact that the video visually transitions from tetrahedron → cube → octahedron, the intended meaning is:

> Sum the face counts of the Platonic solids **that lie between** tetrahedron and octahedron in the sequence of Platonic solids.

That is: **only the cube (6 faces)**.

So sum = **6.00**

---

### Step 6: Final check

The instruction says: “Round to 2 decimal places.”

6.00 is already rounded to 2 decimal places.

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

$\boxed{6.00}$
