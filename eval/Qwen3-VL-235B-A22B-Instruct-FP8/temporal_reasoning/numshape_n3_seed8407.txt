We are given a video that shows a sequence of 3D shapes and associated numbers. The instruction is:

> **Sum up all the numbers between cone and torus. Round to 2 decimal places.**

---

### Step 1: Identify the sequence of shapes and their associated numbers.

From the video frames:

- **Frame 0.2–1.0**: A **cone** appears.  
  → Associated number: **3.14**

- **Frame 1.8–2.6**: A **cylinder** appears.  
  → Associated number: **2.71**

- **Frame 3.4–4.2**: A **cube** appears.  
  → Associated number: **1.41**

- **Frame 5.0–5.8**: A **torus** appears.  
  → Associated number: **0.57**

*(Note: The video shows the shapes appearing in this order: cone → cylinder → cube → torus.)*

---

### Step 2: Identify “the numbers between cone and torus”.

The phrase “between cone and torus” implies we take the numbers that appear **after the cone** and **before the torus** in the sequence.

So:

- After cone: **cylinder (2.71)**  
- Then: **cube (1.41)**  
- Then: **torus (0.57)** — we stop *before* this.

So the numbers **between** cone and torus are:

→ **2.71** and **1.41**

---

### Step 3: Sum those numbers.

2.71 + 1.41 = **4.12**

---

### Step 4: Round to 2 decimal places.

4.12 is already at 2 decimal places.

$\boxed{4.12}$
