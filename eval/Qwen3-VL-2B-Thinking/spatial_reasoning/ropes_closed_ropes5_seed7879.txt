### Key Geometric Insight

For **n lines** in **general position** (i.e., no two lines are parallel, and no three lines intersect at the same point), the number of **bounded regions** (closed shapes) formed is given by the formula:

$$
B(n) = \frac{(n - 1)(n - 2)}{2}
$$

This formula is derived from the fact that each new line intersects all previous lines, and each intersection increases the number of bounded regions it forms.
