- **From word 2 to 3**: M at 6 → M at 1 → $ |1 - 6| = 5 $
- **From word 3 to 4**: M at 1 → M at 1 → $ |1 - 1| = 0 $
- **From word 4 to 5**: M at 1 → M at 6 → $ |6 - 1| = 5 $

Add up all the distances:

$$
5 + 5 + 0 + 5 = 15
