We are given a sequence of four cubes, each with a different color on top after being rolled. The colors are:

- Blue
- Red
- White
- Green
- Yellow
- Purple

We are to count how many times the color **green** was rolled in total.

Looking at the sequence of rolls:

1. First roll: The top face is **blue**.
2. Second roll: The top face is **red**.
3. Third roll: The top face is **white**.
4. Fourth roll: The top face is **green**.

So, green appears **once** in the sequence.

Therefore, the total number of times green was rolled is:

\boxed{1}
