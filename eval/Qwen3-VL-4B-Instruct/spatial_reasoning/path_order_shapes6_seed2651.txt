First, let's list the order in which the arrow visits the shapes:

1. The arrow starts at the yellow triangle (△) — this is the first shape visited.
2. The arrow then moves to the orange triangle (△) — this is the second shape visited.
3. The arrow then moves to the blue circle (○) — this is the third shape visited.
4. The arrow then moves to the red square (□) — this is the fourth shape visited.

Now, we need to determine the color of the 4th shape visited. According to the list above, the 4th shape visited is the red square.

Therefore, the color of the 4th shape to be visited is red.
