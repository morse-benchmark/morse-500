We are given a sequence of arrows pointing to different colored shapes. The task is to determine which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path.

Let’s observe the trajectory of the arrow step by step:

1. The first arrow points from a green circle to a yellow circle. The green circle is the starting point, and the yellow circle is the first shape it points to.
2. The next arrow points from the yellow circle to an orange triangle. So, the orange triangle is the second shape in the path.
3. The next arrow points from the orange triangle to a blue triangle. So, the blue triangle is the third shape in the path.
4. The next arrow points from the blue triangle to a white triangle. So, the white triangle is the fourth shape in the path.
5. The next arrow points from the white triangle to a red triangle. So, the red triangle is the fifth shape in the path.

Now, we need to determine which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path. This means we need to compare the distances between consecutive shapes in the path.

Looking at the positions of the shapes:

- The green circle is at the leftmost position.
- The yellow circle is to the right of the green circle.
- The orange triangle is to the right of the yellow circle.
- The blue triangle is to the right of the orange triangle.
- The white triangle is to the right of the blue triangle.
- The red triangle is to the right of the white triangle.

The distances between consecutive shapes are increasing as we move from left to right. Therefore, the red triangle is the furthest from the previous shape (the white triangle) in the path.

Thus, the answer is: red.
