We are given a sequence of five cubes, each rolling and showing a different color on top. We need to count how many times the color "green" appears on top after all the rolls.

Let's analyze each cube one by one:

1. **First cube**: Initially, the top face is red. After rolling, the top face becomes white. So, green does not appear here.

2. **Second cube**: Initially, the top face is blue. After rolling, the top face becomes green. So, green appears once here.

3. **Third cube**: Initially, the top face is red. After rolling, the top face becomes purple. So, green does not appear here.

4. **Fourth cube**: Initially, the top face is yellow. After rolling, the top face becomes green. So, green appears once here.

5. **Fifth cube**: Initially, the top face is purple. After rolling, the top face becomes green. So, green appears once here.

Now, let's add up the occurrences of green:

- Second cube: 1 time
- Fourth cube: 1 time
- Fifth cube: 1 time

Total = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3

Therefore, the color green was rolled a total of 3 times.
