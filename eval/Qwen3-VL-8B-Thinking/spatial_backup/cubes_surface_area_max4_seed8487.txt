A = 2(3 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 2) = 2(6 + 6 + 4) = 2 \cdot 16 = 32
$$

### Step 3: Verification via Cube Count and Shared Faces

Alternatively, we can confirm this by counting the total number of **exposed faces** of all unit cubes:

- Total number of unit cubes = $ 3 \times 2 \times 2 = 12 $
- Each cube has 6 faces → $ 12 \times 6 = 72 $ total faces
- Shared faces (between adjacent cubes): Each shared face hides 2 faces (one from each cube)
- For a 3×2×2 prism:
  - Horizontal shared faces: $ (3 - 1) \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8 $
  - Vertical shared faces: $ 3 \cdot (2 - 1) \cdot 2 = 6 $
  - Depth shared faces: $ 3 \cdot 2 \cdot (2 - 1) = 6 $
  - Total shared faces = $ 8 + 6 + 6 = 20 $
- Total hidden faces = $ 20 \times 2 = 40 $
- Total exposed faces = $ 72 - 40 = 32 $

This confirms the surface area is **32**.
