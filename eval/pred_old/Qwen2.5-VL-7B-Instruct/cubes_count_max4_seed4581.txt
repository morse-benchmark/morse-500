The structure shown in the video is a 3x3x3 cube made up of smaller cubes. When the cube is rotated and some cubes fall off, we need to count how many cubes are left.

Step 1: Count the total number of cubes in the original 3x3x3 cube.
- The original cube has 3 layers, each with 3 rows and 3 columns.
- Total number of cubes = 3 * 3 * 3 = 27

Step 2: Observe the video and count the number of cubes that fall off.
- In the video, it appears that 6 cubes fall off when the cube is rotated.

Step 3: Subtract the number of fallen cubes from the total number of cubes.
- Number of cubes left = Total number of cubes - Number of fallen cubes
- Number of cubes left = 27 - 6 = 21

Therefore, the number of cubes left is \boxed{21}.
