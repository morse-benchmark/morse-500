To determine how many times blue was rolled in total, we need to count the number of times the blue cube appears on top after each roll.

1. The first roll shows the blue cube.
2. The second roll shows the yellow cube.
3. The third roll shows the green cube.
4. The fourth roll shows the blue cube.
5. The fifth roll shows the purple cube.

Counting the blue cubes:
- First roll: 1 blue cube
- Fourth roll: 1 blue cube

Adding these together:
1 + 1 = 2

Therefore, the blue cube was rolled a total of 2 times.
