To determine which color cube appears most often, let's analyze the structure of the cube shown in the video:

1. The cube is composed of smaller cubes.
2. Each smaller cube has a different color: blue, red, green, and yellow.
3. The cube is rotated and viewed from different angles.

By observing the cube, we can see that each color appears an equal number of times on the visible faces of the cube. Since there are four colors and the cube is symmetrical, each color must appear exactly once on each face when considering all possible rotations.

Therefore, the frequency of each color appearing on the cube is the same.
