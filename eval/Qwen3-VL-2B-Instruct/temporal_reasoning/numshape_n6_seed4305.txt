The problem asks us to calculate the volume of a sphere and a right rectangular prism. We are given the following information:

- The radius of the sphere is 5 units.
- The dimensions of the rectangular prism are 2 units by 3 units by 4 units.

First, let's calculate the volume of the sphere. The formula for the volume of a sphere is:

V_sphere = (4/3)πr³

Plugging in the radius of 5 units:

V_sphere = (4/3)π(5)³
V_sphere = (4/3)π(125)
V_sphere = (500/3)π
V_sphere ≈ 523.5988

Rounding to 2 decimal places, we get:

V_sphere ≈ 523.60

Now, let's calculate the volume of the rectangular prism. The formula for the volume of a rectangular prism is:

V_prism = length × width × height

Plugging in the dimensions:

V_prism = 2 × 3 × 4
V_prism = 24

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is 24.

Now, we need to add the volumes of the sphere and the rectangular prism:

V_total = V_sphere + V_prism
V_total = 523.60 + 24
V_total = 547.60

Therefore, the total volume is approximately 547.60.

The final answer is 547.60.
