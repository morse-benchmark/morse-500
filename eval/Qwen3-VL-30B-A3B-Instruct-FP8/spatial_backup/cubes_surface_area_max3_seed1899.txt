Let's analyze the structure step-by-step to find the surface area.

1. **Count the number of unit cubes:**
   - The structure is made up of 4 unit cubes.
   - We can see that there are 4 cubes in total.

2. **Determine the total surface area if the cubes were separate:**
   - Each unit cube has 6 faces.
   - So, 4 cubes would have a total of \(4 \times 6 = 24\) faces.

3. **Calculate the number of faces that are hidden due to the cubes being joined:**
   - When two cubes are joined, they share a face, and this face is no longer part of the surface area.
   - We need to count how many faces are hidden.

   Let's examine the structure:
   - There are 3 connections between the cubes:
     - One cube is connected to two others.
     - Another cube is connected to one other.
     - The fourth cube is connected to one other.

   - Each connection hides 2 faces (one from each cube).
   - So, there are 3 connections, hiding \(3 \times 2 = 6\) faces.

4. **Calculate the surface area:**
   - Total faces: 24
   - Hidden faces: 6
   - Surface area: \(24 - 6 = 18\)

Therefore, the surface area of the figure is \(\boxed{18}\).
