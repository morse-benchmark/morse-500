Let's analyze the sequence of shapes and their colors as they appear in the video:

1. First, a purple diamond appears.
2. Then, a purple triangle appears.
3. Next, an orange star appears.
4. Finally, a gray square appears.

We are asked to find the color of the shape that appeared 2 turns before the square.

Let's count backward from the square:
- The square is the 4th shape.
- The shape that appeared 1 turn before the square is the orange star (3rd shape).
- The shape that appeared 2 turns before the square is the purple triangle (2nd shape).

So, the color of the shape that appeared 2 turns before the square is purple.

The answer should be in lowercase.

\boxed{purple}
