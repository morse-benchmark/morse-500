Let's analyze the sequence of shapes and their colors as they appear in the video.

1. First, a square appears. It is brown.
2. Next, a diamond appears. It is purple.
3. Then, a hexagon appears. It is green.
4. After that, a circle appears. It is pink.
5. Finally, an octagon appears. It is blue.

We are asked to find the color of the shape that appeared 2 turns before the octagon.

- The octagon is the last shape in the sequence.
- Two turns before the octagon means we need to look at the shape that appeared two steps earlier in the sequence.
- The shape that appeared one turn before the octagon is the circle (pink).
- The shape that appeared two turns before the octagon is the hexagon (green).

Therefore, the color of the shape that appeared 2 turns before the octagon is green.

The answer is \boxed{green}.
