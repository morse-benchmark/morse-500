### Step 1: Understand what "exactly 3 faces exposed" means

In a 3D cube structure, each small cube can have 0 to 3 faces exposed, depending on its position:

- **Corner cubes** (where 3 faces meet) → **3 faces exposed**
- **Edge cubes (not corners)** → 2 faces exposed
- **Face-center cubes** → 1 face exposed
- **Internal cubes** → 0 faces exposed

So, cubes with **exactly 3 faces exposed** are **corner cubes** of the overall structure.
