### Step 1: Understand the problem

Each small cube has 6 faces, each of area 1 square unit. However, when cubes are adjacent, their touching faces are **not** part of the external surface area — they are internal.

So, the total surface area = (number of exposed faces) × 1.

We need to count the number of **exposed faces** of all the cubes.

### Step 4: Use the “exposed faces” method

Instead of counting cubes, we can count the number of exposed faces.

Each cube has 6 faces. If we know the number of cubes and the number of **adjacent pairs** (i.e., faces touching), then:

Total surface area = 6 × (number of cubes) − 2 × (number of adjacent pairs)

Because each adjacent pair hides 2 faces (one from each cube).

So, if we can count the number of cubes and the number of cube-to-cube adjacencies, we can compute the surface area.
