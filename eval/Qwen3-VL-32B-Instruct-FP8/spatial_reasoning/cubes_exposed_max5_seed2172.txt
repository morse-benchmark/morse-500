### Step 1: Understand the problem

A cube in the structure has **exactly 1 face exposed** if **five of its six faces are adjacent to other cubes (or the structure’s interior)**, and **only one face is on the outside**.

This typically happens for cubes that are **completely surrounded on five sides** — for example, in the middle of a face of the entire structure, but not on an edge or corner.

In other words, such cubes are **not on the edges or corners** of the structure — they lie in the **interior of a face** of the overall shape.
