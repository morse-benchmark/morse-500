We are given a cube with colored faces and a path it must follow. We are to determine which color is on top when the cube reaches the red square.

---

### Step 1: Understand the initial cube orientation

At the start (0:07), the cube is shown with three visible faces:

- **Top face**: Red
- **Front face**: Yellow
- **Right face**: Purple

We are told to remember the color names and their corresponding squares:

- Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Orange, Purple

So the cube has six faces, each a different color.

From the initial view, we know:

- Top = Red
- Front = Yellow
- Right = Purple

From this, we can deduce:

- Bottom = Opposite of Top → **Green** (since Red is top, Green is bottom)
- Back = Opposite of Front → **Blue** (since Yellow is front, Blue is back)
- Left = Opposite of Right → **Orange** (since Purple is right, Orange is left)

So the full cube mapping is:

- Top: Red
- Bottom: Green
- Front: Yellow
- Back: Blue
- Right: Purple
- Left: Orange

---

### Step 2: Trace the cube’s movement along the path

The cube moves along a path of tiles. The path is shown as:

```
Start → [Tile 1] → [Tile 2] → [Tile 3] → [Red Square]
```

From 0:15 to 0:22, we see the cube moving along the path. Let’s analyze the movement step by step.

#### Movement 1: First tile (rightward)

The cube rolls **forward** (in the direction of the path) onto the first tile. Since the front face is Yellow, rolling forward means the **front face becomes the bottom face**, and the **bottom face (Green) becomes the back face**, and so on.

But more precisely, when a cube rolls forward (i.e., along the direction of its front face), the **front face becomes the bottom**, the **bottom becomes the back**, the **back becomes the top**, and the **top becomes the front**.

Wait — let’s think carefully.

Actually, when a cube rolls **forward** (toward the direction of the front face), the **front face rotates down** to become the new bottom, and the **top face rotates down** to become the new front.

So:

- Original:
  - Top: Red
  - Front: Yellow
  - Bottom: Green
  - Back: Blue

After rolling forward (onto first tile):

- The **front face (Yellow)** becomes the **bottom**.
- The **bottom face (Green)** becomes the **back**.
- The **back face (Blue)** becomes the **top**.
- The **top face (Red)** becomes the **front**.

So after first roll:

- Top: Blue
- Front: Red
- Bottom: Yellow
- Back: Green
- Right: Purple (unchanged)
- Left: Orange (unchanged)

---

#### Movement 2: Second tile (rightward again)

Now the cube rolls forward again. Current front face is **Red**.

Rolling forward:

- Front (Red) → Bottom
- Bottom (Yellow) → Back
- Back (Green) → Top
- Top (Blue) → Front

So after second roll:

- Top: Green
- Front: Blue
- Bottom: Red
- Back: Yellow
- Right: Purple
- Left: Orange

---

#### Movement 3: Third tile (rightward again)

Current front face: **Blue**

Rolling forward:

- Front (Blue) → Bottom
- Bottom (Red) → Back
- Back (Yellow) → Top
- Top (Green) → Front

So after third roll:

- Top: Yellow
- Front: Green
- Bottom: Blue
- Back: Red
- Right: Purple
- Left: Orange

---

### Step 3: Final position — reaching the red square

The cube has now moved along the path and reached the **red square**.

We are asked: **What color face is on top?**

From the last step, after the third roll:

✅ **Top face = Yellow**

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

\boxed{yellow}
