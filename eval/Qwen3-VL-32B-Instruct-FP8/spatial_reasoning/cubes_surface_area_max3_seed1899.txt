### Step 3: Use the method of counting exposed faces

Each cube has 6 faces. If there are **n** cubes, total faces = 6n.

But whenever two cubes are adjacent, they **hide 2 faces** (one from each cube).

So, surface area = 6n - 2×(number of adjacent pairs).

So we need to:

1. Count the number of cubes (n).
2. Count the number of adjacent pairs (edges where cubes touch).
