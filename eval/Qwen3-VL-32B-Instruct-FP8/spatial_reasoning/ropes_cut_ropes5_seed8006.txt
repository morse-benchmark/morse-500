We are given a scene with multiple colored lines and a single dotted line. The question asks: **How many sections does the dotted line cut the colored lines into?**

---

### Step 1: Understand the question
We are to count the **number of sections** the colored lines are divided into **by the dotted line**. That is, wherever the dotted line intersects a colored line, it splits that colored line into segments (sections). We must count **all** such segments across **all** colored lines.

---

### Step 2: Identify the colored lines
From the video frames (especially at 0:05–0:10), we can observe the following colored lines:

- **White**
- **Purple**
- **Orange**
- **Green**
- **Red**

That’s **5** distinct colored lines.

---

### Step 3: Observe the dotted line
The dotted line is vertical (or nearly vertical) and passes through the center of the scene, from top to bottom.

---

### Step 4: Count how many times the dotted line intersects each colored line

We need to count the **number of intersection points** between the dotted line and each colored line. Each intersection point **splits** a colored line into **one more section**.

For example:
- If a line is intersected **once**, it is split into **2 sections**.
- If intersected **twice**, it is split into **3 sections**.
- In general, **n intersections → n+1 sections**.

So, we need to count the number of intersections per colored line.

---

### Step 5: Analyze each colored line

Let’s go one by one.

#### 1. White line
- It’s a smooth curve, starting from the top-left, going down, then up to the right.
- The dotted line intersects it **once** (in the middle, where the curve dips down).
→ **1 intersection → 2 sections**

#### 2. Purple line
- Starts from bottom-left, curves upward, then downward to the right.
- The dotted line intersects it **once** (near the peak of the curve).
→ **1 intersection → 2 sections**

#### 3. Orange line
- Looks like a long, horizontal oval or ellipse, slightly tilted.
- The dotted line passes through it **twice** (once on the left side, once on the right side).
→ **2 intersections → 3 sections**

#### 4. Green line
- Similar to orange, but slightly different shape.
- The dotted line passes through it **twice** (left and right sides).
→ **2 intersections → 3 sections**

#### 5. Red line
- A complex, wavy line that crosses over others.
- The dotted line intersects it **twice** (once near the top, once near the bottom).
→ **2 intersections → 3 sections**

---

### Step 6: Add up all sections

- White: 2
- Purple: 2
- Orange: 3
- Green: 3
- Red: 3

Total = 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 = **13**

\boxed{13}
