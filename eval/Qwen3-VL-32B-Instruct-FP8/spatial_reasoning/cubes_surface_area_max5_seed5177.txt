### Step 2: Surface area of a single cube

Each cube has 6 faces, each 1x1 = 1 unit².

So, if all 17 cubes were separate, total surface area = 17 × 6 = 102.

But when cubes are joined, **each shared face reduces the total surface area by 2** (since two faces are no longer exposed).

So we need to count the **number of adjacent cube pairs** (i.e., number of shared faces).

---

### Step 3: Count shared faces

We have 17 cubes arranged in two layers:

- Bottom layer: 3x3 grid → 9 cubes
- Top layer: 3x3 grid, but center cube missing → 8 cubes

Let’s count shared faces **within each layer** and **between layers**.

---

#### a) Shared faces within bottom layer (3x3 grid)

In a 3x3 grid, number of adjacent pairs:

- Horizontal adjacencies: 3 rows × 2 = 6
- Vertical adjacencies: 3 columns × 2 = 6
- Total = 12 shared faces

Each shared face reduces surface area by 2 → so 12 × 2 = 24 units less

---

#### b) Shared faces within top layer (3x3 grid minus center)

Top layer has 8 cubes: positions (1,1), (1,2), (1,3), (2,1), (2,3), (3,1), (3,2), (3,3) — center (2,2) missing.

Now count adjacencies:

- Horizontal:
  - Row 1: (1,1)-(1,2), (1,2)-(1,3) → 2
  - Row 2: (2,1)-(2,3) — but (2,2) is missing, so only one adjacency? Wait, (2,1) and (2,3) are not adjacent — no direct adjacency.
    → Actually, (2,1) is adjacent to nothing in row 2 horizontally? Because (2,2) is missing, so (2,1) and (2,3) are not adjacent.
    → So row 2: 0 horizontal adjacencies
  - Row 3: (3,1)-(3,2), (3,2)-(3,3) → 2
  - Total horizontal: 2 + 0 + 2 = 4

- Vertical:
  - Column 1: (1,1)-(2,1), (2,1)-(3,1) → 2
  - Column 2: (1,2)-(2,2) — but (2,2) missing → no adjacency
    (2,2) missing, so (1,2) is not adjacent to (3,2) — no vertical adjacency in col 2
  - Column 3: (1,3)-(2,3), (2,3)-(3,3) → 2
  - Total vertical: 2 + 0 + 2 = 4

Total adjacencies in top layer: 4 + 4 = 8

Each shared face reduces surface area by 2 → 8 × 2 = 16 units less

---

#### c) Shared faces between bottom and top layers

Top layer cubes are placed directly above bottom layer cubes, **except the center cube is missing**.

So, which top cubes are above bottom cubes?

All 8 top cubes are placed above bottom cubes — because only center is missing.

So, 8 vertical adjacencies between layers.

Each shared face reduces surface area by 2 → 8 × 2 = 16 units less

---

### Step 4: Total surface area

Total surface area if separate: 17 × 6 = 102

Total reduction due to shared faces:

- Bottom layer: 12 shared faces → 12 × 2 = 24
- Top layer: 8 shared faces → 8 × 2 = 16
- Between layers: 8 shared faces → 8 × 2 = 16

Total reduction = 24 + 16 + 16 = **56**

So, surface area = 102 - 56 = **46**
