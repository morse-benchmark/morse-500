**Step 3: Answer the question**

> Which color shape was the furthest from the previous shape in the path?

We need to compare the distances between:

- Orange circle → White square
- White square → Green circle

Visually, from the frames:

- The orange circle is on the left side of the screen.
- The white square is near the center.
- The green circle is on the right side.

So, the distance from orange → white is roughly half the screen width (left to center).

The distance from white → green is also roughly half the screen width (center to right).

But visually, the green circle is farther from the white square than the orange circle is — because the green circle is at the far right edge, while the orange circle is near the left edge but not quite at the edge.

Wait — let’s re-examine the frames.

At 0:12: Orange circle on left, white square in center, green circle on right.

At 0:13: Arrow from orange to white.

At 0:14: Arrow from white to green.

The green circle appears to be **farther** from the white square than the orange circle is — because the green circle is near the far right edge, while the orange circle is not at the far left edge (it’s slightly in from the left).

But actually, looking at the positions:

- Orange circle is on the left side, not extreme left.
- Green circle is on the right side, near the extreme right.
- White square is centered.

So, the **distance from white square to green circle** is **greater** than the distance from white square to orange circle.

Therefore, the **green circle** was the furthest from the previous shape (white square).
