### Step 1: Understand the problem

We are told to compute the **sum of the distances of all shifts** that happened to the letter **'D'** across all word moves.

Each frame shows a rearrangement of the letters of "MARYLAND" — the letters are being permuted, not added or removed.

We need to:

- Track the **position** of the letter **'D'** in each step.
- Compute the **absolute difference in position** (i.e., distance) between its position in the current word and the previous word.
- Sum all those distances.

Note: Since the word is always 8 letters long, positions are from 0 to 7 (0-indexed).
