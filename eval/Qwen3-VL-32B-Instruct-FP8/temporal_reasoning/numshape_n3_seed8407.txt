We are given a sequence of 3D shapes appearing one after another, each associated with a number displayed in the top right corner. The shapes and their corresponding numbers are:

1. **Cone** → number: **1.45**
2. **Rectangular prism (box)** → number: **5.67**
3. **Torus (donut shape)** → number: **8.76**

The question asks:  
> **Sum up all the numbers between cone and torus. Round to 2 decimal places.**

---

### Step 1: Understand "between cone and torus"
The cone is the first shape (1.45), and the torus is the third shape (8.76). The phrase “between cone and torus” likely refers to the numbers associated with the shapes that appear **after the cone and before the torus**.

In this case, that is **only the rectangular prism**, which has the number **5.67**.

So, we are to sum **only the number between cone and torus**, i.e., **5.67**.

---

### Step 2: Sum the numbers
There is only one number between cone and torus:  
**5.67**

Sum = 5.67

---

### Step 3: Round to 2 decimal places
5.67 is already to 2 decimal places.

---

### ✅ Final Answer:
\boxed{5.67}
