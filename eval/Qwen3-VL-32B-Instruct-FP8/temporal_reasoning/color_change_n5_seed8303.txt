We are given a sequence of shapes appearing one after another, each for one "turn", and we are asked:

> What was the color of the shape that appeared 3 turns before the oval?

We need to reconstruct the sequence of shapes and their colors, then count backwards 3 turns from the oval.

---

Let’s list the frames in order:

- **Frame 0**: Black screen — no shape.
- **Frame 1**: A **gray pentagon** appears.
- **Frame 2**: A **yellow triangle** appears.
- **Frame 3**: A **teal star** appears.
- **Frame 4**: An **orange circle** appears.
- **Frame 5**: A **green oval** appears.

So, the sequence of shapes (by turn number) is:

- Turn 1: Gray pentagon
- Turn 2: Yellow triangle
- Turn 3: Teal star
- Turn 4: Orange circle
- Turn 5: Green oval

The oval appears on **turn 5**.

We are to find the color of the shape that appeared **3 turns before** the oval.

That is: Turn 5 - 3 = **Turn 2**

On Turn 2, the shape was the **yellow triangle**.

So, the color is **yellow**.
