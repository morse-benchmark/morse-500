We are given a word: **MOUNTAIN**, and we are told that it undergoes several shuffles. The question asks:

> What is the difference in position of the letter **'T'** between **after shuffle 3** and **after shuffle 0**?

We are to return the answer as a number.

---

### Step 1: Understand the initial state — shuffle 0

At shuffle 0, the word is **MOUNTAIN**.

Let’s index the positions starting from 1 (as is common in such problems unless specified otherwise).

So:

Position:   1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8  
Letter:     M  O  U  N  T  A  I  N

We are interested in the letter **'T'**.

In **shuffle 0**, 'T' is at **position 5**.

---

### Step 2: Track the shuffles

We are shown the word after each shuffle. Let’s list them one by one.

#### Shuffle 1:
Word shown: **NTIUOMAN**

Let’s write it with positions:

Position:   1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8  
Letter:     N  T  I  U  O  M  A  N

So, **'T' is at position 2**.

#### Shuffle 2:
Word shown: **UNOMNTAI**

Position:   1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8  
Letter:     U  N  O  M  N  T  A  I

So, **'T' is at position 6**.

#### Shuffle 3:
Word shown: **NTNUOMIA**

Position:   1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8  
Letter:     N  T  N  U  O  M  I  A

So, **'T' is at position 2**.

---

### Step 3: Compute the difference

We are asked for the **difference in position of 'T' between after shuffle 3 and after shuffle 0**.

That is:

> Position after shuffle 3 — Position after shuffle 0

= 2 — 5 = **-3**

But the question says: “Return the answer as a number.” — it doesn’t specify absolute value, so we return the signed difference.

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

\boxed{-3}
