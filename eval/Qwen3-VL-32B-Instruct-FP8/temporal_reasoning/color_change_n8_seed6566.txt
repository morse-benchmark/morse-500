We are given a sequence of shapes appearing one at a time, each on a black background. The question asks:

> What was the color of the shape that appeared 3 turns before the triangle?

We need to count backwards 3 turns from the triangle.

---

Let’s list the shapes in order of appearance, with their colors and turn numbers.

Turn 1: Red hexagon  
Turn 2: Purple circle  
Turn 3: Purple star  
Turn 4: Teal square  
Turn 5: Yellow oval  
Turn 6: Gray diamond  
Turn 7: Blue octagon  
Turn 8: Green triangle ← This is the triangle.

We need the shape that appeared **3 turns before** the triangle.

So, 3 turns before turn 8 is turn 5.

Turn 5: Yellow oval

Therefore, the color of the shape that appeared 3 turns before the triangle is **yellow**.

The question asks to output in **lower case**.

---

✅ Final Answer: **yellow**
