We are given a video sequence showing shapes appearing one after another, and we are to list the **duration** (in seconds) of **each shape from the start of drawing**, in the **order they appeared**, rounded to **1 decimal place**.

---

### Step 1: Identify when each shape appears and disappears.

From the video frames:

- **Frame 0.0s**: Black screen.
- **Frame 1.0s**: A **gray parallelogram** appears.
- **Frame 2.0s**: A **purple triangle** appears to the right.
- **Frame 5.0s**: A **brown star** appears between the parallelogram and triangle.
- **Frame 6.0s**: All shapes are visible. Then the screen goes black at **6.6s**.

So, the shapes appear in this order:
1. Gray parallelogram
2. Purple triangle
3. Brown star

---

### Step 2: Determine the duration of each shape from the start of drawing.

We assume "start of drawing" means the moment each shape **first appears** on screen.

We need to calculate how long each shape is **visible** from its appearance until the screen goes black (at 6.6s).

#### 1. Gray parallelogram:
- Appears at **1.0s**
- Disappears at **6.6s**
- Duration = 6.6 - 1.0 = **5.6s**

#### 2. Purple triangle:
- Appears at **2.0s**
- Disappears at **6.6s**
- Duration = 6.6 - 2.0 = **4.6s**

#### 3. Brown star:
- Appears at **5.0s**
- Disappears at **6.6s**
- Duration = 6.6 - 5.0 = **1.6s**

---

### Step 3: List the durations in the order the shapes appeared.

Order: parallelogram → triangle → star  
Durations: 5.6s, 4.6s, 1.6s

---

### Final Answer:

\boxed{5.6,4.6,1.6}
