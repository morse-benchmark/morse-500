### Step 3: Compute the difference

We are to find the **difference in position of 'E' between after shuffle 3 and after shuffle 0**.

- Position after shuffle 0: **4**

But the question says: **"What is the difference in position..."** — it doesn’t specify order, but typically, "difference between A and B" means **A - B**.

Here, it says: **"between after shuffle 3 and after shuffle 0"** — so it’s **(after shuffle 3) - (after shuffle 0)**.

So:

> **0 - 4 = -4**
