We are given a sequence of shapes appearing one after another, each for one "turn", and we are asked:

> What was the color of the shape that appeared 3 turns before the triangle?

---

Let’s list the shapes and their colors in order of appearance, turn by turn.

---

**Turn 1**: Yellow pentagon  
**Turn 2**: Orange oval (appears after pentagon)  
**Turn 3**: Gray hexagon  
**Turn 4**: Purple octagon (appears after hexagon)  
**Turn 5**: Purple square  
**Turn 6**: Green triangle (appears after square)

---

The triangle appears on **Turn 6**.

We need the shape that appeared **3 turns before** that → Turn 6 - 3 = **Turn 3**

---

**Turn 3**: Gray hexagon

So, the color is **gray**

---

The question says: **Output in lower case**

So, we output: **gray**

---

✅ Final Answer: **gray**
