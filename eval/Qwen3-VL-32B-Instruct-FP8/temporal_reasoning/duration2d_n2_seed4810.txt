---

### Step 1: Identify when each shape appears and disappears.

From the video frames:

- **Frame 3.0s**: Both shapes disappear (screen goes black).

Duration = Time when shape disappears - Time when shape appears.

- **Orange hexagon**:

Answer to 1 decimal point, comma-separated, in order of appearance.
