We are given a visual animation of a blue ball moving horizontally across a black screen, bouncing off vertical gray bars. The question asks:

> **How many seconds did the ball travel before hitting an object?**

We are to **answer in seconds (rounded to 1 decimal place)**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the animation

From the frames:

- At **t=0**, the ball is on the left side, and there is one vertical bar on the right.
- The ball moves right → hits the bar at **t=3**.
- Then it bounces left → hits a new bar on the left at **t=5**.
- Then it bounces right → hits the right bar again at **t=8**.
- Then it bounces left → hits the left bar again at **t=11**.
- Then it bounces right → hits the right bar again at **t=13**.

Wait — but the question says: **"before hitting an object"** — meaning the **first time** it hits an object.

So we need to find the time from **start (t=0)** until the **first collision**.

---

### Step 2: Identify the first collision

From the frames:

- At **t=0**: ball is on the left, no collision yet.
- At **t=3**: ball is touching the right bar → **first collision**.

So the ball traveled for **3 seconds** before hitting an object.

---

### Step 3: Round to 1 decimal place

3 seconds → already at 1 decimal place: **3.0**

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

\boxed{3.0}
