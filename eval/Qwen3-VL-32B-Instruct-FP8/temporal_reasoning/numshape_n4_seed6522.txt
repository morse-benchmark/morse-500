We are given a sequence of frames showing 3D shapes and numbers appearing on a black background. The task is:

> **Sum up all the numbers between sphere and rectangular prism. Round to 2 decimal places.**

---

### Step 1: Identify the order of events

Let’s list the frames in order and note what appears:

- **Frame 0.0–1.0**: Black screen → then a **sphere** appears at 1.0 seconds.
- **Frame 2.0**: A number **“4.76”** appears.

### Step 2: Determine which numbers lie **between** the sphere and the rectangular prism

- The **sphere** appears at **1.0 seconds**.
- The **rectangular prism** appears at **6.0 seconds**.

So, we need to sum **all numbers that appear between 1.0 and 6.0 seconds** (i.e., after the sphere and before the rectangular prism).

Let’s list the numbers appearing **after the sphere (1.0s)** and **before the rectangular prism (6.0s)**:

- **Frame 2.0**: “4.76” → ✅
- **Frame 4.0**: “0.01” → ✅
- **Frame 5.0**: “7.14” → ✅

Note: The cylinder (3.0s) and the numbers at 7.0s and 8.0s come **after** the rectangular prism (6.0s), so they are **not included**.

Also, the cube at 9.0s and 4.95 at 10.0s are after the rectangular prism — excluded.

### Step 3: Sum the numbers

We sum:

> 4.76 + 0.01 + 7.14

Calculate:

- 4.76 + 0.01 = 4.77
- 4.77 + 7.14 = **11.91**

11.91 is already to 2 decimal places.
